# Factions Overview

A guide to the dynamic faction ecosystem in the FSM mod.

## Core Vision: Choice Shapes Destiny

In FSM, players forge their own allegiances. Your reputation dictates:
- **Allies & Foes:** Factions have deep-seated historical friendships and bitter feuds (*beef*).
- **Available Quests:** Quests are tied directly to your allied faction's objectives.
- **Boss Encounters:** Aligning with the Hearthbound Accord puts you on the hunt for Viking warlords and rogue mages. Backing the Northmen or dark forces turns the Accord's grand castles and paladins into your siege targets.
- **Ability Unlocks:** Factions grant unique power trees: Northmen teach rune magic and battle fury; the Accord trains in holy defense and mounted mastery; the Archmage unlocks multi-elemental and cosmic spells; dark forces teach lifesteal and necromancy.
- **Moral Alignment:** No forced heroism. Players can openly ally with dark cults or undead forces to raze settlements to the ground.

## Diplomatic Conflicts & Alliances

### 1. Northmen vs. Hearthbound Accord (The Border War)
- **The Beef:** Northmen send raiders and longships down rivers into fertile plains to raid Accord villages for food, cattle, and metal. The Accord views them as savage invaders and executes raiders on sight.
- **Gameplay Impact:** Siding with the Northmen turns village guards and knights hostile. Assisting the Accord makes you a prime target for Berserkers and scouting parties.

### 2. Hearthbound Accord vs. Archmage Faction (Heretics & Terrors)
- **The Beef:** The Archmage was once the revered protector of all villages until his experimental obsession tore open realities and created uncontrollable elemental monsters. The Accord actively hunts his disciples and seeks to purge his laboratories.
- **Gameplay Impact:** Working with the Archmage unlocks forbidden elemental and cosmic spells, but makes you an outlaw across civilized villages.

### 3. All Factions vs. The Undead (Or Become Their Harbinger)
- **The Beef:** Undead forces threaten all living creatures. Both vikings and knights must constantly defend their borders against night terrors.
- **The Dark Path:** Players who desire chaotic destruction can ally with necromantic cabals, betray living settlements, and lead hordes to wipe out civilized towns.

## Reputation Systems

- **Every Action Matters:** Killing faction members, defending settlements, or completing contracts directly shifts your faction standing.
- **Banners & Ranks:** Advance from an untrusted drifter to a decorated champion, unlocking exclusive armor, banners, and warband recruits.
- **Ability Exclusivity:** Committing deeply to one school of power locks out opposing ability trees.

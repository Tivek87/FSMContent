# Hearthbound Accord

> Protectors of civilization, order, and village peace

## Overview

The **Hearthbound Accord** is a disciplined chivalric faction dedicated to guarding villages, securing trade highways, and pushing back monstrous incursions. They cooperate closely with iron golems and resident village spellcasters.

## Faction Mobs

All Accord defenders are documented in the mob catalog:

| Mob | Role | Combat Style | Documentation |
|-----|------|--------------|---------------|
| Soldier Footman | Town guard | Sword & oval shield patrolman | [Soldier Footman](../mobs/hearthbound-accord/soldier-footman.md) |
| Knight | Chivalric elite | Mounted paladin with two-handed halberd | [Knight](../mobs/hearthbound-accord/knight.md) |

## Village Defense Dynamics

- **Soldier Footmen** walk regular patrol routes through major settlements.
- **Knights** command village garrisons from horseback and can blow warhorns to rally reinforcements.
- Striking down an Accord Knight incurs severe faction penalties across all allied villages.

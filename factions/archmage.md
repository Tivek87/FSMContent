# Archmage Faction

> Unstable elemental amalgams, cosmic horrors, and warped disciples

## Overview

The faction centered around the renegade Archmage. Encounters with his creations are rare throughout the wilderness, but each engagement represents a high-intensity battle against complex, multi-elemental and cosmic magic.

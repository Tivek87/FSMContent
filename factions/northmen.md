# Northmen Faction

> Viking warriors of the frozen frontiers

## Overview

The **Northmen** are a resilient faction inhabiting snowy biomes (taigas, frozen peaks, and snowy plains). They establish custom viking villages and deploy formidable raiding warbands into warmer river valleys.

## Spawn Locations

- **Villages:** In snowy biomes (see [Viking Villages](../structures/northmen/viking-villages.md)).
- **Camps & Ships:** Fortified encampments along riverbanks and forests (see [Fortified Camps](../structures/northmen/fortified-camps.md)) and raiding longships.

## Village Inhabitants (NPCs)

- **Northmen Villagers:** Specialized profession holders (farmer, fisherman, weaponsmith, woodworker, hunter, shipbuilder, and the rare Völva mystic). They trade using various treasures and gems (not merely emeralds) and can actively fight back when threatened.

## Reputation & Banner Allegiances

Player standing depends on interactions with individual warbands. Different camps fly unique banners; gaining renown with one jarl may influence standing across the entire faction.

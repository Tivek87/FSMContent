# Viking Villages

> Custom Northmen settlements in snowy biomes

## Overview

Custom viking settlements generating in snowy taigas and frozen plains. Featuring wooden longhouses, craft workshops, and fortified perimeters, these villages house diverse Northmen profession holders.

---

## Settlement Buildings

| Structure | Spawn Condition | Purpose |
|-----------|-----------------|---------|
| **Farmer's Longhouse** | Always | Agricultural food stores |
| **Fisherman's Hut** | Near water | Ice fishing and boat gear |
| **Weaponsmith** | Always | Weapon forging and repair |
| **Woodworker** | Trees nearby | Timber and palisade crafting |
| **Hunting Lodge** | Always | Furs, meat, and bow supplies |
| **Shipbuilder** | Sufficient water | Longship construction dock |
| **Völva Shrine** | Very rare | Mystical sanctuary with potions and runic gems |

---

## Settlement Inhabitants

- **Northmen Villagers:** Trade with amber, pelt, and precious metals. Unlike vanilla villagers, they will take up arms to defend their homes.
- **Viking Raiders:** Garrison sentinels patrolling settlement borders.
- **Iron Golems & Apprentice Wizards:** Occasionally bolster defenses in the largest settlements.

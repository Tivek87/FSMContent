# Fortified Camps

> Northmen Military Encampments in Forests and Plains

## Overview

Fortified Camps generate in temperate forests, plains, and riverbanks. They serve as forward bases for Northmen raiding parties, ranging in scale from minor scouting outposts to sprawling leader strongholds.

---

## Scouting Warbands

Encampments periodically deploy scouting parties to patrol surrounding lands:

| Camp Tier | Patrol Size | Patrol Range | Leader Joins? |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|---------------|
| **Small** | 1–3 | 100 blocks | No |
| **Medium**| 5–7 | 200 blocks | No |
| **Large** | 8–10 | 300 blocks | No (remains in camp) |
| **Leader**| 12–15 | 500 blocks | Yes (commands the party) |

### Patrol Dynamics

- Scouts subtract from the camp's defenders, temporarily leaving the base more vulnerable to assault.
- Patrols remain active for 4 in-game days and return on day 5.
- Encampments roll a chance to deploy scouting parties every 5 in-game days.

## Specialized Camp Defenses & Unit Distribution

Northmen encampments assign specialized roles across the new tactical units:
- **Watchtowers & Palisade Corners:** Manned by [War-Horn Lookouts](../../mobs/northmen/war-horn-lookout.md) and [Frost-Tipped Huntsmen](../../mobs/northmen/frost-huntsman.md).
- **Perimeter Gates & Breaches:** Guarded by [Shieldmaidens](../../mobs/northmen/shieldmaiden.md) and patrolling [War-Hound Handlers](../../mobs/northmen/war-hound-handler.md).
- **Supply Tents & Hearth:** Protected by [Rune-Etched Hearth-Guards](../../mobs/northmen/hearth-guard.md).
- **Camp Forges:** Maintained by the [Runesmith War-Forger](../../mobs/northmen/runesmith-warforger.md).
- **Command Pavilions:** Shielded by [Seithr-Bound Huscarles](../../mobs/northmen/seithr-huscarle.md) guarding the [Party Leader](../../mobs/northmen/party-leader.md).
- **Water Palisades & Piers:** Guarded by [Longship Harpooners](../../mobs/northmen/longship-harpooner.md).
- **Siege Depots (Leader Camps):** House [Battering Ram Crews](../../mobs/northmen/ram-crew.md) and [Blood-Sworn Flailers](../../mobs/northmen/bloodsworn-flailer.md).

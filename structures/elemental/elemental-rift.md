# Elemental Rift

> Extremely rare world structure — Endgame / post-Archmage content

## Overview

When elemental magic becomes so concentrated and unstable in one place, it tears open a **rift in reality**. These rifts are the ultimate consequence of the Archmage's reckless experiments — places where the boundary between the normal world and something else has broken.

## Appearance

- A visible tear or crack in the air, glowing with chaotic elemental energy
- Swirling mix of all elemental colors
- The area around the rift is visually corrupted/distorted
- Particles and energy leak from the rift

## Spawn

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Rarity | Extremely rare |
| Location | Anywhere, but more likely near areas with high elemental activity |
| Type | World-generated structure |

## Behavior

- Elemental enemies **continuously spawn from the rift**
- The enemies are aggressive and varied (wraiths, corrupted mages, elemental creatures)
- The rift keeps spawning until it's depleted or the player leaves the area

## Loot

After clearing the enemies, the rift can be **looted** for:
- **Cosmic items** — Rare crafting materials from beyond reality
- Random cosmic drops per rift
- Multiple rift clears needed to collect enough for crafting

## Cosmic Weapons

Cosmic items from rifts can be combined to craft **legendary weapons**:
- Unique abilities tied to cosmic/void energy
- Top-tier power level
- Require parts from multiple different rifts

> Exact cosmic weapon designs TBD.

## Design Notes

- This is late-game / post-Archmage content
- Rifts are meant to feel dangerous and otherworldly
- The cosmic weapon grind gives endgame players a long-term goal
- Rifts are a natural extension of the "too much elemental magic = bad" theme
- Could tie into a potential cosmic/void questline in the future

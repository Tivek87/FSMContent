# Shattered Laboratory

> Abandoned underground research complex of the Archmage

## Overview

The **Shattered Laboratory** is a rare subterranean complex that generates deep within stone caverns or beneath towering mountain ranges. This site served as one of the clandestine workshops where the Archmage conducted his earliest multi-elemental experiments prior to his descent into cosmic obsession.

---

## Visual Design & Architecture

- **Layout & Structure:**
  - A sprawling network of cracked stone brick corridors, reinforced with rusted copper piping and basalt support arches.
  - Features high-ceilinged containment vaults centered around fractured containment pedestals.
- **Materials & Textures:**
  - Cracked and mossy deepslate bricks, oxidized cut copper, polished basalt, and reinforced iron grates.
  - Shattered glass canisters containing residual glowing alchemical slime and crystallized magma pools.
- **Atmosphere & VFX:**
  - Periodic electrical arcs jumping between dangling copper wires.
  - Patches of frozen permafrost immediately adjacent to boiling lava drainage channels, illustrating the lab's unstable climate.

---

## Inhabitants

- **[Corrupted Mages](../../mobs/elemental/corrupted-mage.md):** Wandering researchers consumed by residual cosmic energy.
- **[Elemental Unstable Golems](../../mobs/elemental/elemental-unstable-golem.md):** Broken security automata patrolling the central chambers.
- **[Surge Sprites](../../mobs/elemental/surge-sprite.md):** Swarming inside exposed electrical conduits.

---

## Notable Loot & Rewards

- **Alchemical Schematics:** Blueprints required to construct the [Elemental Infusion Altar](../../systems/elemental-infusion.md).
- **Inscribed Research Journals:** Cryptic entries revealing the lore of the Archmage's madness and coordinate hints toward [Elemental Rifts](elemental-rift.md).
- **Pristine Crystal Conduits:** Vital crafting components for high-tier elemental weaponry.

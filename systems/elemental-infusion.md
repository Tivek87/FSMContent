# Elemental Infusion Altar

> Crafting and progression workbench for elemental armaments and equipment

## Overview

The **Elemental Infusion Altar** is a specialized workbench enabling players to imbue standard weaponry, shields, and armor with permanent or interchangeable elemental attributes.

---

## Construction & Requirements

- Crafted using polished deepslate, copper conduits, a central runic core, and elemental essence obtained from elemental mobs.
- Must be placed in a room with adequate elemental stability or nearby elemental catalysts (e.g. lava source, packed ice, or lightning rods).

---

## Mechanics: Imbuing Gear

Players insert an eligible item alongside an elemental catalyst into the altar:

| Element | Weapon Imbue | Armor Imbue |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| **Fire** | Incinerating Strikes (applies Burning) | Flame Retardance & Thorns Ignition |
| **Ice** | Frostbite Edge (inflicts Slowness & Freezing) | Permafrost Plating (slows melee attackers) |
| **Lightning** | Arc Discharge (chains damage to nearby foes) | Static Charge (repels close attackers upon hit) |
| **Earth** | Crag Cleave (heavy knockback & shield disable) | Tectonic Density (massive knockback resistance) |
| **Wind** | Gale Thrust (extended reach & launch power) | Featherstep (fall damage reduction & leap boost) |

---

## Multi-Elemental Fusion

Advanced altars found in [Shattered Laboratories](../structures/elemental/shattered-laboratory.md) permit fusing two complementary elements into a single piece of equipment (e.g., Fire + Lightning for Plasma Weaponry).

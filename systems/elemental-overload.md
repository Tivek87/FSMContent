# Elemental Overload System

> High-risk, high-reward magical feedback mechanic for elemental combat

## Overview

The **Elemental Overload** system ensures elemental spellcasting and abilities remain strategic rather than endlessly spammable. Channeling elemental sorcery generates internal thermal and arcane strain.

---

## The Overload Meter

- Every cast of an elemental ability or swing of an infused weapon fills a portion of the player's **Overload Meter**.
- The meter decays naturally over time when abstaining from spellcasting or through specific alchemical draughts.

---

## Thresholds & Penalties

| Threshold | State | Effect |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| **0% - 50%** | Stable | Normal ability usage; standard cooldowns. |
| **51% - 85%** | Volatile | Spell damage increased by +15%, but ability cooldowns increase by +25%. |
| **86% - 99%** | Critical | Spells trigger chaotic minor backlashes (self-inflicted slowness, static shocks). |
| **100%** | **Overload Detonation** | The player violently detonates with elemental energy, taking heavy unblockable damage and suffering an Arcane Burnout debuff (silencing all elemental abilities for 10 seconds). |

---

## Strategic Counterplay

- Alternating between opposing elements (e.g., casting an Ice spell immediately after a Fire spell) releases excess thermal energy safely without filling the Overload Meter.

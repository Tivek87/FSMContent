# Mobs Catalog

Comprehensive index of all creatures, faction fighters, world bosses, and NPCs within the FSM mod.

## Northmen Units (`mobs/northmen/`)
- [Viking Raider](northmen/viking-raider.md) — Frontline warrior with 4 tunic variants, painted shields, axe, and hunting bow.
- [Berserker](northmen/berserker.md) — Dual-axe berserker with 4 bear-pelt variants, ignoring damage and sundering shields.
- [Party Leader](northmen/party-leader.md) — 2.5-block commander with 4 sacred war banners, spangenhelm, and battle axe.
- [Rune-Galdr](northmen/rune-galdr.md) — Elite mystic deploying rune shields, lifesteal pools, and reactive teleportation.
- [Skald](northmen/skald.md) — Travelling war poet bolstering warband morale with frame drum rhythms and resistance chants.
- [Runesmith War-Forger](northmen/runesmith-warforger.md) — Heavy runic smith imbuing ally armaments and slamming anvil fissures (A lot of magic).
- [Seithr-Bound Huscarle](northmen/seithr-huscarle.md) — Chieftain royal guard wielding an aurora broadsword and reactive ice armor (Mid magic).
- [Rune-Etched Hearth-Guard](northmen/hearth-guard.md) — Scale-armored camp defender erecting defensive golden arrow domes (Mid magic).
- [Frost-Tipped Huntsman](northmen/frost-huntsman.md) — Tower sniper firing frostbite arrows and laying glacial snares (Lightly magic).
- [War-Hound Handler & Fen-Hound](northmen/war-hound-handler.md) — Beastmaster pair with anti-invisibility runic collars and takedown bites (Lightly magic).
- [Blood-Sworn Flailer](northmen/bloodsworn-flailer.md) — Fanatical shocktrooper wielding ignited spinning chain flails (Lightly magic).
- [Shieldmaiden Shield-Breaker](northmen/shieldmaiden.md) — Heavy frontline warrior with a tower shield and shield-disabling war pick (No magic).
- [War-Horn Lookout](northmen/war-horn-lookout.md) — Camp sentry blowing a great horn that alerts and mobilizes the entire base (No magic).
- [Longship Harpooner](northmen/longship-harpooner.md) — Whaling hunter reeling in distant targets with rope and shattering boats (No magic).
- [Battering Ram Crew](northmen/ram-crew.md) — Two-man siege breacher unit splintering fortified gates and trampling defenders (No magic).
- [Seidr-Weaver](northmen/seidr-weaver.md) — High-magic fate sorceress weaving soul links, spirit-bears, and rime-storms.
- [Runebound Champion](northmen/runebound-champion.md) — Mid-magic greatsword weapon-master wielding bog-iron blades and runic dashes.
- [Barrow Sentinel](northmen/barrow-sentinel.md) — Light-magic crypt guardian with frost thrusts and an invisibility-piercing soul-fire lantern.
- [Huscarl](northmen/huscarl.md) — Non-magical shield-wall juggernaut with an impenetrable tower shield and war-pick.
- [War-Howler](northmen/war-howler.md) — Primal wolf handler commanding 2 leashed Tundra War-Hounds in flanking pincer tactics.
- [Longship Harpooner](northmen/longship-harpooner.md) — Nautical reaver dragging distant targets into melee with heavy barbed whaling harpoons.
- [Northmen War-Troll](northmen/northmen-war-troll.md) — 3.6-block chained siege giant hurling glacial boulders and swinging pine trunks.
- [Drengr Duelist](northmen/drengr-duelist.md) — Non-magical agile blade master executing sword and seax counter-parries.
- [Jarl of the Frozen Fjord](northmen/jarl.md) — 2.3-block clan warlord miniboss wielding the frost broadaxe Fjord-Biter and commanding huscarls.

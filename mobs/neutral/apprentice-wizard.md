# Apprentice Wizard

> Neutral — Village spellcaster and defender

## Overview

The **Apprentice Wizard** spawns within major villages as an arcane defender assisting Iron Golems and Soldier Footmen. Neutral toward players, they defend the settlement against raiders and monsters, but will retaliate if the player harms villagers.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Humanoid villager model (1.95 blocks tall, long nose) with **two separate mobile arms** (unlike standard joined villager sleeves).
  - Features an aged, flowing gray beard that sways realistically with movement.
  - Dressed in long, flowing arcane robes with split sleeves and wide cuffs in one of three elemental schools: **White** (Holy), **Red** (Fire), or **Blue** (Ice).
  - Visible elemental sparks and runes gather in their palms before flying as magical bolts.

---

## Elemental Variants

### 1. White Wizard (Holy / Light)
- **Passive Aura:** Grants **Regeneration I** to nearby villagers and allies (including players with high village reputation).
- **Light Bolt:** Fires an illuminating bolt dealing moderate damage and applying the **Glowing** effect.
- **Light Burst:** Radial flash of light stunning surrounding enemies for 2 seconds.

### 2. Red Wizard (Fire)
- **Passive Aura:** Grants **Strength I** to nearby defenders.
- **Fireball:** Hurls non-destructive fireballs that burn targets without creating fire blocks on the ground (preventing village destruction).
- **Fire Circle:** Radial burst of flame setting adjacent hostiles on fire without burning timber blocks.

### 3. Blue Wizard (Ice)
- **Passive Aura:** Grants **Resistance I** to nearby allies.
- **Frost Bolt:** Launches ice shards inflicting **Slowness**.
- **Mini Blizzard:** Radial subzero storm applying **Weakness** to surrounding enemies.

---

## Trading Mechanics

- Trades specialize in potions, enchantments, and catalysts matching their elemental school.
- Currency uses rare elemental materials (e.g. blaze rods, breeze rods) rather than standard emeralds.
- Maxing trade renown with an Apprentice Wizard can provide key catalysts for forging [Elemental Weapons](../../items/elemental-weapons.md).

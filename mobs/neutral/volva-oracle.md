# Völva Oracle

> Northmen Faction — Village seeress, herbalist & aurora guardian (Mid-High Magic)

## Overview

The **Völva Oracle** is the sacred spiritual anchor of Northmen settlements. Residing inside the rare Völva magic houses within snowy villages, she interprets celestial omens, brews restorative draughts, and protects the village perimeter with luminous auroral enchantments drawn from the Northern Lights.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Humanoid elder female figure (1.8 blocks tall), draped in flowing ceremonial robes.
  - Carries a consecrated copper galdr staff crowned with amber beads and dried alpine herbs.
- **Attire & Materials:**
  - Robes woven from natural undyed fleece, bordered with woven tablet-bands in vibrant saffron and woad-blue.
  - Wears a necklace of pierced wolf teeth, raw amber nuggets, and fossilized bog-wood beads.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Soft, undulating ribbons of green and violet auroral light (*The Aurora Veil*) dance gently above her shoulders.
  - Sweet, resinous smoke from dried juniper and sage continuously drifts from her belt brazier.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Sits cross-legged inside her shrine when not disturbed, stirring an alchemical bronze cauldron.
  - When defending her home, lifts her staff with both hands to cast radiant light domes over fleeing villagers.

## Abilities & Sacred Magic

### 1. Aurora Aegis (Sanctuary Dome)
Channels the Northern Lights into an expanding emerald-and-violet light dome (6-block radius):
- Grants **Regeneration II** and **Resistance I** to all friendly Northmen villagers and players with high reputation.
- Repels incoming undead and arrows, bouncing projectiles off the outer membrane.

### 2. Omen of the Norns (Prophetic Insight)
Casts an ancestral gaze upon an attacking enemy:
- Places an ethereal glowing eye above the player for 8 seconds; the player's next melee strike will 100% miss or glance harmlessly off armor due to prophetic foresight.

### 3. Juniper Purge
Scatters burning embers from her herbal brazier in front of her, blinding and staggering hostile attackers for 2 seconds.

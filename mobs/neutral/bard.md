# Bard

> Neutral — Traveling village minstrel & showman

## Overview

The **Bard** is an eccentric wandering minstrel who performs in bustling village taverns, marketplaces, and civic squares. Clad in colorful town doublets, they entertain villagers and weary adventurers with jaunty melodies and tales of distant realms.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Villager build (1.95 blocks tall, classic villager profile) with two mobile, articulated arms.
  - Wears a teal and gold-trimmed velvet doublet, soft leather turnshoes, and a feathered cap.
- **Instruments & Gear:**
  - Wears a back harness holding three classical instruments:
    - **Gittern:** A small plucked lute with a carved pegbox.
    - **Boxwood Flute:** Slender woodwind for lively jigs.
    - **Violin & Bow:** Carved spruce fiddle played under the chin.
  - Sends colorful musical note particles swirling through the air when performing.

---

## Stats & Baseline Attributes

| Attribute | Value | Details |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| **HP** | Low (20 HP / 10 hearts) | Performer, not a warrior |
| **Armor** | 4 (Fine leather doublet) | Tailored for mobility |
| **Hostility** | Neutral (Peaceful) | Never attacks first |
| **Spawn** | Large villages, taverns, and town squares | Solitary wanderer |

---

## Behavior & Interactions

- **Morale Melody (Support):** Playing music near villagers and players with high village reputation grants **Speed I** and minor saturation regeneration.
- **Evasion:** If attacked by raiders or hostile players, the Bard immediately flees toward the nearest Iron Golem or Soldier Footman while sounding an alarm.
- **Trading:** Sells sheet music, music discs, and exotic woodwind instruments for emeralds.

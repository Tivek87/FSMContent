# Soldier Footman

> Hearthbound Accord — Standard village guard

## Overview

The **Soldier Footman** serves as the primary protector of Accord settlements. Patrolling village streets alongside iron golems and resident spellcasters, they maintain civic security and repel barbarian or monster incursions.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Standard humanoid build (1.8 blocks tall).
  - Steel chainmail worn beneath a heraldic tabard with flowing front and back flaps displaying Accord insignia.
  - Features an open-faced kettle hat helmet, steel pauldrons, and a leather belt with an iron buckle.
- **Garrison Posts & Variants:**
  - **Village Footman:** Standard tabard, patrolling town streets.
  - **Harbor Watch:** Reinforced waterproof leather boots, stationed near docks.
  - **Farmstead Militia:** Lighter chainmail, guarding perimeter crops and livestock.
  - **Keep Guard:** Polished steel accents, standing sentry at manor gates.
- **Armaments:**
  - Steel arming sword and a tear-shaped oval shield bearing the Accord crest.

---

## Stats & Baseline Attributes

| Attribute | Value | Details |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| **HP** | Moderate (24 HP / 12 hearts) | Standard guard unit |
| **Armor** | 8 (Chainmail harness & kettle hat) | Reliable physical defense |
| **Resistance** | Shield Block | Absorbs projectile and melee blows |
| **Hostility** | Accord Reputation Based | Defends allied settlements |
| **Spawn** | Large villages and fortified Accord outposts | Patrol pairs or squads |

---

## Combat Behavior

- **Melee (Disciplined Guard):** Advances with shield raised, delivering disciplined sword slashes and thrusts with a small chance to inflict Bleed.
- **Teamwork:** Coordinates with nearby Iron Golems and Apprentice Wizards, holding frontline aggro while mages cast from behind.

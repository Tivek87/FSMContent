# Knight

> Hearthbound Accord — Chivalric mounted defender

## Overview

The **Knight** is an elite champion of the Hearthbound Accord. Renowned throughout civilized territories, killing an Accord Knight incurs severe reputation penalties with all allied settlements. They patrol major settlements and highway networks, frequently mounted atop armored warhorses.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Tall humanoid warrior (1.9 blocks tall) fully encased in polished steel plate armor: cuisses, knee cops, greaves, sabatons, and broad flared pauldrons.
  - Wears a closed greathelm with a cross-slit brass visor (only the determined eyes show through the slit).
  - Draped in a tailored cloth surcoat with front heraldic flaps and a flowing cape fastened with golden brooches.
  - Wields a long steel halberd featuring extended melee reach, with a sheathed arming sword at the hip.
- **The Four Chivalric Orders:**
  - **Village Knight:** Blue and silver heraldry, patrolling town squares.
  - **Royal Guard:** Gold and crimson tabards, defending high-tier keep manors.
  - **Black Knight:** Midnight-tinted steel plate, patrolling contested wilderness roads.
  - **White Order:** Polished silver and white cloth, specialized in hunting undead and rogue mages.
- **Mount (Armored Warhorse):**
  - Accompanied by a heavy warhorse equipped with leather, chainmail, or iron barding with matching heraldic caparisons.
  - In villages, the horse is held on a lead; on highways, the knight patrols mounted.

## Abilities & Combat

### 1. Halberd Cleave
Performs a sweeping frontal arc slash with extended reach:
- Cleaves multiple targets, applying a **Bleeding** debuff.
- Blocking negates damage but **temporarily disables the player's shield**.

### 2. Lance Charge (Mounted)
When mounted, gallops forward at high velocity, delivering an impaling thrust dealing damage scaled to current riding speed.

### 3. Influence (Call to Arms)
Blows an Accord signaling horn to summon **2 Soldier Footmen** (mounted if the knight is mounted).

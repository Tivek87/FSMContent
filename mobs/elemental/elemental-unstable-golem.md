# Elemental Unstable Golem

> Hostile — Rare cavern & mountain miniboss

## Overview

The **Elemental Unstable Golem** is a cracked stone construct held together by volatile elemental magic. Its stones float apart, bound only by spiralling wisps of elemental spirit, and one arm is missing entirely, replaced by a churning vortex of fire, frost, wind, and lightning.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Hulking, asymmetric stone golem standing 3.2 blocks tall and 2.4 blocks wide.
  - Composed of floating stone fragments that never physically touch, each bobbing smoothly in the joint gaps.
  - **Hollow Core:** Features a hollow chest of five stone blocks enclosing a free-flying, glowing elemental core.
  - **Arms:** A cracked deepslate fist on the left arm; a roaring, multi-colored elemental vortex of spinning loose stone fragments on the right.
- **The Four Elemental Natures:**
  - **Unstable:** Shifting rainbow cracks cycling between all elements.
  - **Magma:** Basalt plates veined with glowing lava, dropping embers.
  - **Glacier:** Permafrost granite encrusted with translucent ice crystals.
  - **Overgrown:** Ancient mossy cobble laced with glowing spore-roots.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Erratic footfalls cause subtle screen shake.
  - Moves with unpredictable acceleration spikes and abrupt directional pivots.

## Passives

### 1. Unstable Movement
Moves with unpredictable acceleration spikes and abrupt directional pivots, making ranged precision difficult.

### 2. Shattering Frame (Below 50% HP)
When reduced below half health:
- Body fractures further, reducing its physical melee damage.
- Jagged stone shards break off and orbit its body violently.
- Every 1 to 5 seconds, it automatically flings loose stone spikes at nearby players like shrapnel.

---

## Active Abilities

### 1. Dual Ground Slam
- **Stone Fist Slam:** Slams its stone fist down → minor shockwave, light knockback, and physical damage.
- **Vortex Slam:** Slams its vortex arm into the earth → moderate shockwave inflicting elemental splash damage.

### 2. Vortex Vacuum
Aims its elemental vortex arm toward the player, channeling an intense wind vortex that drags the target straight toward its stone fist before delivering a follow-up melee strike.

### 3. Overcharged Lightning Sweep
Performs a horizontal arm sweep. Mid-swing, an arc of lightning extends from its fist, **doubling its attack reach** and discharging chain lightning upon contact.

### 4. Thunder Clap
Slams both hands together with monumental force, dealing heavy concussive knockback and discharging a ring of spiked stone fragments in all directions.

### 5. Death Blast (Elemental Core Failure)
Upon death, the golem's elemental containment core fails. It freezes motionless for 1.5 seconds before **violently exploding** in a massive elemental nova.

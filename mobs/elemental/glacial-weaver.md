# Glacial Weaver

> Hostile — Floating frost specter spinning subzero snares

## Overview

The **Glacial Weaver** drifts through frozen glacial caverns and snowy peaks. Unlike natural spiders, it is an ethereal, floating arachnid spirit created by the Archmage to weave strands of freezing permafrost.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - An eerie floating arachnid-like body measuring 1.6 blocks across, suspended 0.5 blocks above the floor.
  - Six elongated, translucent icicle legs extend downward like delicate needles.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - Constructed of pristine, translucent cyan glacier ice with an opaque white permafrost core.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Emits falling snowflakes and cold vapor mist.
  - Leaves patches of thin ice where it hovers near water or snow.

## Active Abilities

### 1. Frost Web (Subzero Snare)
Spits a projectile of crystallized ice silk:
- Creates a 3x3 patch of frost webs on impact that immobilize players and apply the **Freezing** cold damage effect.

### 2. Icicle Barrage
Fires sharp icicles from its needle-legs that deal piercing physical damage and apply **Slowness I**.

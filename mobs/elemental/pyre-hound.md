# Pyre Hound

> Hostile — Volcanic canine pack predator

## Overview

The **Pyre Hound** is a quadrupedal beast forged from volcanic magma and igneous basalt. Breeding in geothermal caves and volcanic rifts, these hounds hunt in relentless packs, overwhelming prey with coordinated burn assaults.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Lean canine silhouette 1.6 blocks long and 1.0 block high, with an arched spine and sharp basalt shoulders.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - Coarse black basalt plates over an internal skeletal frame of glowing molten lava.
  - Eyes and mouth burn with white-hot flame; drools liquid fire.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Constant trail of rising embers and dark smoke puffs.
  - Ignites flammable blocks upon walking over them.

---

## Active Abilities

### 1. Molten Bite
Lunges forward with burning jaws, dealing initial physical damage and applying a 5-second **Burning** debuff.

### 2. Pack Ignition (Howl)
The alpha hound howls, igniting the bodies of all pack members within 10 blocks, granting them **Speed I** and a fiery damage aura.

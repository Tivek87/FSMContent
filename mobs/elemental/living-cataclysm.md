# Living Cataclysm

> Miniboss — Colossal levitating titan of magma, permafrost, and thunder

## Overview

The **Living Cataclysm** manifests where volatile elemental nexus points rupture against alpine terrain. It is a walking meteorological disaster: a towering elemental titan that simultaneously wields volcanic eruptions, glacial blizzards, and hurricane-force thunderstorms.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - A monumental, legless humanoid torso standing **4.2 blocks tall and 2.8 blocks across the shoulders**.
  - Its lower torso tapers into a swirling, funnel-shaped vortex of soot, hail, and spinning boulders that propels the titan forward.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - **Right Arm (Infernal Side):** Thick plates of black basalt veined with boiling, molten magma. The right hand is an oversized fist of dripping lava.
  - **Left Arm (Glacial Side):** Razor-sharp plates of blue permafrost and dense glacier ice, terminating in jagged icicle claws.
  - **Chest & Helm:** A carved volcanic crag featuring hollow eye sockets glowing with blinding white lightning.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Arcs of lightning continuously crackle across the titan's chest between its fire and ice shoulders.
  - The vortex beneath its torso sucks in loose foliage, dust, and gravel.
  - Heat mirages ripple along its right flank, while dense freezing fog envelopes its left.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Drifts with deliberate, unstoppable momentum.
  - Raises both fists high into the air during attack windups, causing its elemental shoulders to expand and blaze with light.

## Passive: Thermal Shock

The central mechanic of the Living Cataclysm:
- Hitting a player with ice attacks applies the **Brittle** status (slowing movement and stiffening armor).
- Following up with a magma attack while Brittle triggers a **Thermal Detonation**, dealing triple damage and temporarily stripping armor effectiveness.

---

## Active Abilities

### 1. Dual Fist Slam (Thermal Sundering)
Slams both fists simultaneously into the terrain:
- Splits the ground: one hemisphere erupts into fire and lava, while the other coats in slippery blue ice.
- Players in the epicenter are launched skyward.

### 2. Lightning Gale (Vortex Vacuum)
Expands its lower vortex into an 8-block radial storm:
- Drags all entities inward toward the grinding center.
- After 2 seconds of channeling, calls down 3 simultaneous lightning bolts upon targets caught in the vortex.

### 3. Hail & Cinder Volley
Unleashes a sweeping frontal barrage of burning volcanic boulders and piercing icicles over a 20-block range.

# Elemental Rift-Siphon

> Hostile — Parasitic dimensional hunter of elemental energy

## Overview

The **Elemental Rift-Siphon** emerges from the unstable fringes of active [Elemental Rifts](../../structures/elemental/elemental-rift.md). Lacking a fixed elemental identity, this entity acts as a living arcane conduit, draining elemental affinities from its environment and opponents and converting them into lethal offensive weaponry.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - A slender, levitating insectoid/alien silhouette measuring 2.1 blocks high and 0.8 blocks wide.
  - Possesses four multi-jointed crystal arms ending in needle-like claws; lower body tapers into an ethereal crystalline stinger.
  - Head lacks eyes or a mouth; its chest contains a **yawning black singularity** (a swirling miniature black hole) that gently pulses.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - Body crafted from translucent, milky-white quartz crystal riddled with obsidian micro-fractures.
  - Four elemental crystal shards orbit its torso:
    - Ruby shard (Fire)
    - Sapphire shard (Ice)
    - Topaz shard (Lightning)
    - Emerald shard (Earth/Nature)
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Absorbing an element illuminates its internal quartz veins with that element's vibrant color.
  - The singularity in its chest creates subtle gravitational lens distortion on nearby particles.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Drifts with erratic, dragonfly-like hovering dashes.
  - Aligns its orbiting shards in front of its chest to focus channeled attacks.

## Passive: Adaptive Carapace

Whenever the Siphon takes elemental or potion-based damage:
- Reconfigures its crystalline shell to match the incoming damage type.
- Gains **100% immunity** to that element for the following 8 seconds.
- Forces players to cycle through different weapons, elements, and tactics.

---

## Active Abilities

### 1. Arcane Extraction (Buff & Vitality Drain)
Locks onto a player with a siphoning beam from its chest singularity:
- Strips active beneficial potion effects (e.g. Strength, Speed) and applies them to itself.
- If no buffs are present, continuously drains player health to recharge its orbiting shards.

### 2. Siphon Volley (Four-Element Salvo)
Fires its four orbiting crystal shards in rapid succession:
- **Shard 1 (Fire):** Ignites target with burning damage.
- **Shard 2 (Ice):** Applies heavy Slowness.
- **Shard 3 (Lightning):** Chains damage to adjacent companions.
- **Shard 4 (Earth):** Delivers heavy physical knockback.

### 3. Dimensional Shift (Defensive Phase)
When struck in close-quarters melee, flickers out of reality and instantaneously reappears 6 blocks behind the attacker.

# Surge Sprite

> Hostile — Swarming static electricity entity

## Overview

The **Surge Sprite** is a diminutive, highly erratic entity of condensed lightning and static energy. Drawn magnetically toward metallic objects, swarms of Surge Sprites aggressively target players wearing iron, copper, or netherite armaments.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - A small hovering orb (0.5 x 0.5 blocks) with four twitching, needle-like electric sparks radiating outward.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - Blinding yellow-white electrical core surrounded by a hazy blue static aura.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Emits loud electric crackles and tiny lightning arcs that ground into nearby metallic blocks.

---

## Stats & Properties

| Attribute | Value | Details |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| **HP** | Very Low (12 HP / 6 hearts) | Extremely fragile |
| **Armor** | 0 | Pure energy |
| **Element** | Lightning / Magnetism | Swarming and disruption |
| **Spawn** | High voltage caves, exposed copper veins, thunderstorms | Swarms of 4–8 |

---

## Active Abilities

### 1. Magnetic Latch
Dashes instantly at players wearing metallic armor:
- Latches onto the player, dealing low continuous electrical shock and temporarily increasing the player's weapon swing cooldowns by 30%.

### 2. Static Discharge
Upon death, detonates in a small spark burst that shocks all adjacent entities.

---

## Drops & Rewards

- **Static Spark:** Used to charge copper batteries and lightning rods.

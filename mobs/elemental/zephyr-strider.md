# Zephyr Strider

> Hostile — Slender towering spirit of high winds

## Overview

The **Zephyr Strider** is an elegant, towering entity of living currents inhabiting mountain passes and open sky realms. It moves with graceful levitation, using turbulent air cushions to deflect projectile barrages.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - A tall, slender silhouette standing 3.2 blocks high with elongated limbs and a streamlined torso that fades into a trailing vortex of wind.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - Semi-translucent pale cyan and white air streams held together by polished quartz rings around its wrists, neck, and waist.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Violent wind swirls kick up dust particles in a 4-block radius around its path.

---

## Active Abilities

### 1. Arrow Deflection Vortex (Passive)
Naturally repels 80% of incoming physical arrows and bolts, swirling them into harmless trajectories.

### 2. Cyclone Stride
Dashes forward in a straight line, picking up players caught in its path and hurling them into the air (causing severe fall damage).

# Glacier Shatterer

> Hostile — Behemoth of permafrost and tectonic granite

## Overview

The **Glacier Shatterer** is a lumbering bipedal siege titan created from living mountain granite and prehistoric permafrost. It bulldozes through player fortifications and regenerates damaged limbs using ambient ice.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - A towering bipedal brute standing 3.8 blocks high and 2.2 blocks wide, with oversized stone forearms.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - Dark granite bedrock overlaid with thick, jagged sheets of pale cyan glacier ice.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Heavy frost vapor billows from its back; leaves icy footsteps that slow pursuing players.

---

## Active Abilities

### 1. Avalanche Charge
Lowers its granite shoulders and charges forward at high speed, shattering player shields and breaking through wooden fortifications.

### 2. Glacial Regeneration (Passive)
In subzero biomes or when standing in snow, slowly regenerates HP and re-crystallizes shattered armor plates.

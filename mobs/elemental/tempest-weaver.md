# Tempest Weaver

> Hostile — High-altitude tri-elemental storm entity

## Overview

The **Tempest Weaver** floats in the upper stratosphere where freezing cloud currents collide with violent electrical storms. It commands the triad of Wind, Lightning, and Ice, striking from the skies with pinpoint precision.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - An ethereal, six-winged seraphic silhouette floating 2.8 blocks tall with a slender energy core.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - Pale silver cloud ribbons, deep indigo storm mist, and translucent icicle spines.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Lightning arcs dance between its three pairs of wings; hail constantly rains beneath it.

---

| Attribute | Value | Details |

| **HP** | Moderate-High (68 HP / 34 hearts) | Airborne spellcaster |
| **Armor** | 8 | Storm ward |
| **Elements** | Wind, Lightning & Ice | Tri-elemental sky dominance |
| **Spawn** | Mountain summits above Y=180 during thunder and snow storms | Solitary |

---

## Active Abilities

### 1. Thunderhead Bolt
Fires a freezing lightning bolt that chains between targets and inflicts both **Slowness** and electrical damage.

### 2. Downburst Gale
Presses the air downward with crushing atmospheric pressure, pinning players to the ground for 3 seconds.

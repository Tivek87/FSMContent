# Frostfire Aberration

> Hostile — Volatile fusion of Fire & Ice

## Overview

The **Frostfire Aberration** is one of the Archmage's most disturbing creations: a violent amalgamation of two opposing elements forced into unnatural symbiosis. Locked in perpetual thermal conflict, molten flame and subzero glacier ice constantly struggle for dominance within its shifting form.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - An asymmetric bipedal beast standing 2.4 blocks tall.
  - The right half is heavily muscled with bulging igneous rock and magma, while the left half is sharp, angular, and encased in crystalline glacier ice.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - Magma fissures pulse bright orange-red on the right; deep permafrost blue plates glisten on the left.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Intense steam plumes billow continuously from the central seam where fire and ice collide.
  - Rapidly shifts between glowing embers and frost particle trails.

## Active Abilities

### 1. Thermal Shockwave
Slams both fists together, unleashing a blast of superheated steam:
- Deals heavy mixed magic damage, knocking back foes while applying both **Burning** and **Slowness**.

### 2. Dual Comet
Launches a paired projectile of a blazing fireball intertwined with a subzero frost shard.

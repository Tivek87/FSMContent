# Elemental Blaze

> Miniboss — Rare subterranean cave encounter

## Overview

The **Elemental Blaze** is an elusive miniboss dwelling within deep caverns and abandoned mineshafts. Unlike standard Nether blazes, this entity was engineered by the Archmage to harness and channel all five primal elements simultaneously.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Central Core & Helm:**
  - The hovering head and core are carved from dark, smoked obsidian-like stone laced with luminous fault lines.
  - An internal multi-colored flame constantly shifts hues within the core.
  - Two piercing eye slits glow brightly in the color of the element currently being prepared for attack.
- **The Five Elemental Rods:**
  - Instead of standard yellow blaze rods, **five distinct orbital rings of rods** revolve around its body, each exhibiting unique textures and particle effects:
    - **Fire Rod:** Molten magma stone dripping with sparks and flame particles.
    - **Ice Rod:** Pointed, translucent glacier crystals emitting cold subzero vapor.
    - **Earth Rod:** Coarse, moss-covered basalt and granite rods shedding sand and pebble motes.
    - **Wind Rod:** Translucent, swirling ribbons of compressed air leaving miniature cyclone trails.
    - **Lightning Rod:** Crackling, ionized rods of azure electrical energy arcing with static sparks.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Rods orbit in concentric rings at varying heights around its core.
  - Prior to launching an attack, the associated rod halts its orbit, snaps forward toward the target, and pulses intensely before releasing its spell.

## Stats & Baseline Attributes

| Attribute | Value | Details |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| **HP** | Low-Moderate | Compensated by active resistances |
| **Defense** | Light Resistance | Flat damage reduction across all incoming hits |
| **Difficulty** | Mid-Tier | Balanced for mixed Iron and Diamond equipment |
| **Spawn** | Rare, subterranean caverns only | Solitary encounter |

---

## Abilities

Each orbital rod grants one distinct elemental ability:

| Element | Ability | Effect |
|---------|---------|--------|
| **Ice** | *Frost Ray* | Fires a concentrated ice beam that freezes the target for several seconds. |
| **Earth** | *Rock Slam* | Pulls a boulder from the floor and hurls it at the player with high velocity. |
| **Lightning** | *Chain Lightning* | Discharges an arc of lightning that chains to nearby allies and inflicts Weakness. |
| **Fire** | *Fireball* | Detonates on impact, leaving burning patches of ground. |
| **Wind** | *Gust Push* | Unleashes a concussive air blast delivering medium knockback and light damage. |

---

## Drops & Rewards

- Drops **1 random Elemental Rod** upon defeat (equal chance for each of the 5 elements).
- Collecting all 5 unique rods enables the player to forge the legendary [Elemental Weapon](../../items/elemental-weapons.md).

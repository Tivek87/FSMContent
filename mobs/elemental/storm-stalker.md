# Storm Stalker

> Hostile — Avian tempest predator of wind and lightning

## Overview

The **Storm Stalker** roosts upon high mountain peaks and hunts across open valleys during thunderstorms. Forged from volatile stormwinds and raw electrical discharge, it ambushes prey with diving lightning strikes.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - A predatory avian wyvern silhouette with a 3.5-block wingspan and a lean 1.8-block body length.
  - Possesses razor-sharp talon feet of dark fulgurite (fused lightning-glass).
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - Feathers consist of dark slate-gray stormclouds edged with bright electric gold filaments.
  - Beak and talons are carved from translucent, branching fulgurite glass.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Miniature lightning arcs continuously jump between its wingtips and tail feathers.
  - Generates faint rain and ozone mist in a 2-block radius around its body.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Circles high above targets before folding its wings and diving at supersonic speeds.

## Active Abilities

### 1. Diving Thunderbolt
Dives from altitude at blinding speed, impacting the target with an explosive lightning discharge.
- Deals direct shock damage and arcs to nearby entities.

### 2. Gale Buffet
Beats its cloud-wings forward, unleashing a directional blast of air that launches targets 6 blocks backward.

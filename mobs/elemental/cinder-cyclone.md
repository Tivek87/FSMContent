# Cinder Cyclone

> Hostile — Airborne vortex of ash, embers, and wind

## Overview

The **Cinder Cyclone** is an airborne funnel of living flame and suffocating volcanic ash. Engineered by the Archmage by compressing a blazing heart into an air spirit, it scours mountain canyons with blistering speed.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - An inverted funnel vortex standing 3.2 blocks tall and 1.6 blocks wide at the crown.
  - At the eye of the vortex hovers a carved obsidian skull mask with glowing ember eyeholes.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - Swirling dark charcoal ash, spinning fire cinders, and black obsidian mask.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Thick smoke plumes trail in its wake; sheds burning spark particles.

## Active Abilities

### 1. Ashen Vortex (Engulf)
Sweeps over players, lifting them inside its burning vortex and inflicting suffocating smoke and continuous fire damage.

### 2. Ember Barrage
Expels volleys of spinning firebrands outward in all directions.

# Tectonic Crawler

> Hostile — Subterranean armored earth predator

## Overview

The **Tectonic Crawler** is a massive earthen beast inhabiting the deepest caverns and tectonic trenches. Constructed from living slate, granite, and sharp quartz crystals, it scuttles on four stout stone limbs and burrows effortlessly through solid bedrock.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - A broad, low-slung, quadrupedal reptilian/beetle-like frame measuring 2.2 blocks wide, 1.4 blocks tall, and 2.6 blocks long.
  - Maintains a low center of gravity resembling an armored primeval lizard.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - Body plates forged from chiseled deepslate and coarse granite, studded with glowing raw copper and amethyst clusters along its spine.
  - Underside protected by smooth, layered shale plates.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Emits falling dust and pebble particles whenever it moves or turns.
  - Amethyst clusters along its back pulse with faint violet light in complete darkness.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Crawls with a slow, grinding gait across floors, but can climb vertical cliff walls and cavern ceilings.
  - Vibrates vigorously before initiating a burrowing dash.

## Active Abilities

### 1. Bedrock Tremor (Earthquake Slam)
Slams its front stone claws into the earth:
- Sends a rolling shockwave through the floor up to 8 blocks in front of it.
- Deals heavy damage and disables player shields for 3 seconds.

### 2. Ceiling Ambush
Clings to high cavern ceilings and drops down upon unsuspecting targets:
- Crushes the landing zone, dealing fall damage proportional to height and causing **Slowness II**.

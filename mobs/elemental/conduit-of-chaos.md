# Conduit of Chaos

> Hostile — Tri-masked hovering apparition of unstable sorcery

## Overview

The **Conduit of Chaos** is the tragic remnant of a catastrophic ritual conducted by the Archmage, wherein three senior arcanists were subjected to an overloaded elemental vortex. Their forms and minds fused into an ethereal entity that embodies the chaotic intersection of Fire, Ice, and Lightning.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - A levitating, spectral upper torso draped in torn ceremonial vestments standing 2.6 blocks high.
  - Protruding from beneath its broad cowl are **three illuminated masks**:
    - **Left Mask:** Carved from frost-blue crystal weeping streams of liquid nitrogen mist.
    - **Center Mask:** Forged from glowing bronze and scorched basalt, with blazing eye slits.
    - **Right Mask:** Chiseled from vibrant amethyst arcing with crackling purple-white lightning.
  - Accompanied by **three disembodied, floating hands** that circle its torso, each holding an elemental focus orb.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - Robes woven from liquid arcane energy, shifting in a gradient from crimson at the hem to deep violet and glacial blue at the cowl.
  - Lower hem frays into wisps of magical smoke and ethereal embers.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Each mask generates distinct environmental particles: frost crystals (left), floating ash (center), and electrical ozone sparks (right).
  - The masks flare with light when chanting or unleashing combination spells.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Glides with imposing, spectral grace at player eye level.
  - Its three floating hands make intricate arcane somatic gestures while weaving incantations.

## Passive: Omnidirectional Vigilance

With three faces looking in distinct directions:
- Cannot be ambushed or backstabbed (immune to sneak attack criticals).
- Detects players through walls within a 16-block radius.

---

## Active Abilities (Fusion Sorcery)

### 1. Frostfire Lance (Fire + Ice Fusion)
The left (Ice) and center (Fire) hands merge their focus orbs, hurling a twin-spiraled lance:
- Deals heavy magic damage and applies both **Burning** and **Slowness II**.
- The player burns while movement is heavily penalized, complicating evasive maneuvers.

### 2. Chained Frostbolt (Ice + Lightning)
Fires a freezing lightning bolt that chains between nearby targets:
- Freezes targets in place for 2 seconds while chaining electrical damage to all adjacent allies.

### 3. Tri-Cast Nova (Desperation Burst)
Upon dropping below 50% HP, all three hands rise simultaneously:
- Discharges a 360-degree radial barrage:
  - 6 bouncing fireballs igniting the ground.
  - 6 piercing icicles dealing sharp damage.
  - 6 lightning strikes called down randomly across the arena.
- Features a 2-second telegraph warning players to take cover behind solid walls.

# Aether-Chimera

> Hostile — Quadruped biological-elemental apex predator

## Overview

The **Aether-Chimera** stands as one of the Archmage's most horrific biological experiments. Attempting to breed an obedient warhound, he bound four primal elemental spirits into the mutated husk of a predatory beast. The resulting creature is an uncontrollable amalgam of volcanic magma, glacial ice, jagged obsidian, and storm winds.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - A heavy quadruped predatory silhouette (blending wolf and sabertooth traits), measuring 2.8 blocks in length, 1.4 blocks in width, and 1.8 blocks at shoulder height.
  - Features heavily armored obsidian forequarters, a hunched feline spine, and twin tattered storm-cyclone wings sprouting from its shoulder blades.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - **Forequarters & Chest:** Heavy plates of rough black obsidian and basalt, with bright molten orange lava glowing through deep fissures.
  - **Hindquarters & Flanks:** Dense coats of azure-tinted packed ice and razor permafrost spines replacing organic fur.
  - **Skull & Jaws:** A chiseled basalt skull; right jaw drips with viscous orange lava, while the left exhales pale blue freezing mist.
  - **Tail:** A long, segmented whip composed of crystallized glacial ice.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Black volcanic smoke rises continuously from its thoracic plates.
  - Wings consist of localized miniature cyclones sparking with yellow electric arcs.
  - Leaves alternating trails of burning embers and frosted ground beneath its paws.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Paces with heavy, rhythmic strides that cause subtle screen shake.
  - Crouches low to the earth before leaping, spreading its storm wings wide with an audible air crack.

## Passive Mechanics

### 1. Dual-Core Temperature Balance
Direct Fire and Ice attacks suffer a -50% damage reduction:
- Ice attacks are absorbed by its molten magma core.
- Fire attacks are quenched by its glacial permafrost flanks.
- Raw physical weaponry (axes, heavy swords) remains the optimal counter.

---

## Active Abilities

### 1. Magma Pounce (Fire + Earth)
Leaps up to 10 blocks through the air, slamming down with basalt claws:
- Fractures the earth in a 3-block impact radius, igniting magma pools on the ground.
- Knocks players flat with heavy physical and fire damage.

### 2. Glacial Tail-Sweep (Ice)
Performs an instantaneous 180-degree spin, whipping its crystallized tail:
- Inflicts **Slowness III** for 3 seconds and has a 50% chance to immediately disable raised shields.

### 3. Tempest Roar (Wind + Lightning)
Rears up on its hind legs, releasing a concussive shockwave roar:
- Blows all targets backward by 8 blocks in a wide frontal cone.
- Summons an immediate lightning strike upon the position where the player stood when the roar began.

---

## Tactical Strategy

- Never fight the Chimera with your back to a cliff or wall; its pounce and roar combos can instantly corner you.
- Focus ranged attacks on the exposed sinew between its obsidian shoulder plates.

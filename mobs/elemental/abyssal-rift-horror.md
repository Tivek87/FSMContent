# Abyssal Rift Horror

> Miniboss — Aberration of shattered elements and cosmic void

## Overview

The **Abyssal Rift Horror** is a grotesque entity that pulled itself through the widest dimensional tears created by the Archmage. It is not a natural creature of the Overworld, but an unstable anomaly fusing chaotic elemental matter with the devouring hunger of the deep void.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - A shifting, amorphous mass measuring 3.5 blocks tall and 3.0 blocks wide, supported by multiple twisting elemental pseudopods.
  - At its core lies a pulsating violet cosmic eye surrounded by hovering void shards.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - Pitch-black void matter veined with crackling purple, orange, and cyan elemental energies.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Pulls in floating gravity particles; distorts the ambient light around its body into deep twilight.

## Active Abilities

### 1. Singularity Collapse
Channels a gravitational collapse in the center of the arena, dragging all players inward before detonating with massive chaos damage.

### 2. Chaotic Barrage
Discharges a simultaneous radial storm of fireballs, frost shards, and void beams.

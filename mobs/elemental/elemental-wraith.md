# Elemental Wraith

> Hostile — Very rare nocturnal aerial entity

## Overview

The **Elemental Wraith** is one of the Archmage's earliest failed experiments: a flying, ghost-like specter composed of swirling, unstable elemental energy. Unable to maintain a physical vessel, it drifts through the night skies as a volatile aerial threat.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Levitating, translucent specter standing approx. 1.8 blocks tall with a trailing spectral tail instead of legs, and a tattered ethereal cape streaming from the tail.
  - **Hooded Visage:** An ominous hollow hood featuring slanted slit eye lights with white-hot cores, needle fangs, and a hanging jaw that remains perpetually slightly ajar.
- **The Four Storm Variants:**
  - **Chaos Wraith:** Rapidly shifts through all primary elemental hues.
  - **Tempest Wraith:** Indigo and gold, sparking with static ozone.
  - **Blight Wraith:** Emerald and sickly yellow, dripping poisonous mist.
  - **Rime Wraith:** Pale cyan and white, shedding dense frost crystals.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Leaves a faint trail of glowing elemental embers across the night sky and casts soft ambient light.

## Attack: Chaos Projectile

The wraith discharges a single volatile projectile resembling a blended ball of all elemental energies. **Players cannot discern which elemental effect it carries until it impacts**:

| Random Outcome | Effect |
|----------------|--------|
| **Fire** | Ignites the target with burning damage. |
| **Ice** | Freezes the target, applying heavy Slowness. |
| **Nature** | Poisons the target for several seconds. |
| **Wind** | Inflicts severe knockback and vertical launch. |
| **Lightning** | Inflicts Weakness and chains static shock to adjacent companions. |

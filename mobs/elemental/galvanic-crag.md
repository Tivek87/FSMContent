# Galvanic Crag

> Hostile — Magnetic earth & lightning brute

## Overview

The **Galvanic Crag** is a hulking quadrupedal construct forged from magnetic iron ore and raw storm discharge. It generates powerful magnetic fields that pull armored warriors toward its crushing stone fists.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - A massive, gorilla-like quadruped stance standing 2.5 blocks tall and 2.0 blocks wide.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - Dark raw iron blocks, magnetic lodestone, and basalt laced with glowing gold conductive wiring.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Constant blue-yellow electrical arcs jumping across its shoulder crags.
  - Magnetic distortion waves visibly warp the air around its fists.

## Active Abilities

### 1. Magnetic Attraction (Passive / Active)
Pulls players wearing iron, copper, or chainmail armor toward itself, disrupting positioning.

### 2. Overload Ground Pound
Slams both magnetic fists into the ground, detonating an electrical earthquake that stuns targets within 5 blocks.

# Corrupted Mage

> Hostile — Arcane researchers twisted by dark cosmic sorcery

## Overview

The **Corrupted Mage** represents the fallen vanguard of the Archmage's former academic circle. Once scholarly apprentices and arcanists from village academies, their prolonged exposure to unshielded dimensional rifts and catastrophic cosmic failures shattered their minds. They wander ruined workshops, ancient crypts, and rift boundaries as fanatical conduits of **dark purple void magic, cosmic dust, and gravitational collapse**.

---

## Visual Design & Cosmic Appearance

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Humanoid build standing 1.95 blocks tall with an unnerving, weightless posture.
  - Their feet hover 0.1 to 0.2 blocks above the earth; arms hang loosely with sudden gravitational twitches.
- **Cosmic Vestments & Robes:**
  - Draped in tattered, layered robes of midnight-black, royal purple, and deep indigo fabric.
  - The interior lining and trailing hems display a **living cosmic nebula texture**: moving star clusters, glowing constellations, and swirling violet-and-indigo cosmic dust drifting continuously across the fabric.
  - The tattered edges dissolve into faint plumes of dark purple smoke rather than ordinary cloth fibers.
- **Cosmic Anatomy (Head & Eyes):**
  - Beneath the ragged cowl, the face is sunken into a deep, star-filled cosmic void with no discernible nose or mouth.
  - **Eyes:** Two blazing, miniature violet stars shine as glowing pupils, surrounded by dark purple coronal flares.
- **VFX & Cosmic Details:**
  - Forearms and hands are stained dark obsidian, wrapped in deep violet nebular haze with tiny orbiting starlight particles (resembling miniature solar systems).
  - Floating fragments of dark crystalline star-matter orbit their shoulders.
  - Leaves a subtle trail of fading violet stardust across the ground while moving.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Glides with smooth, inertial levitation rather than walking leg animations.
  - When casting, raises both hands as their hood flares with starlight, drawing dark purple cosmic matter into a swirling focal orb above their palms.

## Abilities & Cosmic Sorcery

### 1. Astral Singularity (Primary Ranged Attack)
Compresses cosmic matter into a spinning, dark purple star-orb and hurls it toward the target.
- **Impact:** Explodes upon contact with a flash of violet cosmic dust, dealing moderate magic damage.
- **Gravitational Pull:** Leaves behind a miniature gravitational anomaly for 1.5 seconds, pulling nearby players 2 blocks toward the center.

### 2. Starfall Salvo (Area Barrage)
Channels energy upward, opening three localized tears in the night sky or ceiling directly above the player:
- Three dark violet comet shards streak down in rapid succession.
- Each impact causes a burst of cosmic sparks and applies **Void Sickness** (disables natural health regeneration for 4 seconds).
- **Telegraph:** Violet astrological circles appear on the floor 1 second before the shards strike.

### 3. Cosmic Warp (Defensive Blink)
When pressured within 4 blocks by melee weapons:
- Collapses into a shower of purple stardust, instantaneously reappearing 7 blocks away.
- Leaves behind a **Stardust Mine** that detonates if the player steps through the origin point, causing blindness and knockback.

### 4. Gravity Distortion Aura (Passive Ward)
- Arrows and ranged projectiles fired at the Corrupted Mage have their flight paths bent by local gravity, suffering a 30% velocity reduction and slight trajectory deflection.

## Void-Touched Arch-Disciple (Rare Elite Variant)

In proximity to active [Elemental Rifts](../../structures/elemental/elemental-rift.md), players may encounter an ascended variant:
- Robes are entirely transparent windows into deep outer space.
- Wields a two-handed hovering **Cosmic Astrolabe** that casts twin singularities.
- Explodes upon defeat into an expanding supernova shockwave that pushes all entities away.

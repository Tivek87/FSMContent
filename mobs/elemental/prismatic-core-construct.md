# Prismatic Core Construct

> Hostile — Floating polyhedral mechanism of rotating elemental rings

## Overview

The **Prismatic Core Construct** is a levitating polyhedral apparatus initially engineered by the Archmage as an elemental accumulator designed to stabilize the volatile cores of his Elemental Blazes. Overwhelmed by excessive magical saturation, the device attained a fractured, aggressive sentience. It now drifts through subterranean ruins and abandoned arcane workshops, discharging cycling elemental resonances.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - A central hovering regular dodecahedron measuring approx. 1.2 x 1.2 x 1.2 blocks, enveloped by **three independently rotating rings** of stone segments (total diameter approx. 2.4 blocks).
  - Hovers permanently 1.0 to 1.5 blocks above floor level; possesses no biological limbs.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - Outer rings are composed of polished, chiseled dark deepslate inlaid with glowing gold and copper circuit-glyphs.
  - The central crystalline polyhedron continuously cycles internal core glows: blazing orange (Fire), vibrant cyan (Ice), electric gold-white (Lightning), and jade green (Wind).
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Arcs of crackling static and elemental motes jump continuously between the revolving rings and the core prism.
  - Emits atmospheric particles reflecting its active phase: floating cinder flakes during fire mode, frost mist during ice mode, and ozone sparks during lightning mode.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - In an idle state, all three rings rotate smoothly in alternating directions.
  - Prior to discharging attacks, the rings freeze abruptly, align their planar faces toward the target, and vibrate vigorously as the core pulses blindingly.

## Passive Mechanics

### 1. Resonance Rotation
Every 12 seconds, the core shifts its active elemental resonance:
- **Fire Phase:** Immune to fire and lava; melee strikes against the construct ignite the attacker.
- **Ice Phase:** +4 Armor rating; emits an aura inflicting Slowness I on nearby entities.
- **Lightning Phase:** +40% flight speed; discharges retaliatory spark arcs upon receiving physical damage.
- **Wind Phase:** Deflects incoming arrows and projectile attacks with a 60% deflection chance.

---

## Active Abilities

### 1. Prismatic Fusion Beam (Channeled Beam)
Locks onto a target and channels a 2-second continuous energy beam combining two elements based on current cycle:
- **Fire + Lightning (Plasma):** Heavy direct magic damage, leaving trails of burning ground.
- **Ice + Wind (Blizzard Gale):** Extreme knockback and applies Freezing cold screen effects.
- **Telegraph:** A visible optical tracking ray forms 1 second prior to firing, giving players time to break line-of-sight.

### 2. Tri-Elemental Nova (Close-Quarters Discharge)
Triggers when a player closes within 3 blocks:
- Ring 1 emits an outward radial fire wave (Burn status).
- Ring 2 unleashes an expanding ring of frost (Slowness II for 4s).
- Ring 3 triggers a concussive air blast delivering 6 blocks of knockback.

### 3. Shard Volley
Launches 3 to 5 crystalline fragments from the rotating rings that detonate upon impact with random elemental debuffs.

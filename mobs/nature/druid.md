# Druid

> Hostile — Camouflaged woodland ambusher

## Overview

The **Druid** is a hostile nature-aligned entity inhabiting dense foliage biomes such as primeval forests, dark oak woods, and swamps. Blending seamlessly with surrounding bark and moss, they set snare traps for unwary travelers.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Humanoid frame (1.8 blocks tall) wrapped entirely in living brambles, twisted wood, and thick moss.
  - Can fold into a bush or stand stock-still as a tree trunk, with its glowing eye-slits going completely dark until an ambush is sprung.
- **Adaptive Biome Camouflage:**
  - Bark and foliage coloration dynamically matches the local biome (dark oak, birch, jungle, or swamp hues).

---

## Stats & Baseline Attributes

| Attribute | Value | Details |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| **HP** | Moderate (36 HP / 18 hearts) | Wood and root structure |
| **Damage** | Low base, high against rooted targets | Ambush specialist |
| **Spawn** | Dense forests and swamps only | Solitary ambush |
| **Exclusions** | Never spawns in deserts, plains, or rocky peaks | Foliage dependent |

---

## Combat & Ambush Mechanics

### 1. Root Snare Ambush
If the Druid detects the player before being spotted:
- Casts a root trap from hiding: grasping briars erupt from the earth, immobilizing the player.
- Trapped players must strike the roots to break free (**axes are most effective**).
- The Druid emerges from concealment, dealing **amplified critical damage** against rooted targets.

### 2. Nature's Mending
Channels ambient plant life to restore its own health during extended skirmishes.

### 3. Spotting the Druid Early
If a player spots the Druid and attacks from range with a bow before the ambush triggers, the Druid is **prevented from casting the root snare**, rendering the fight significantly easier.

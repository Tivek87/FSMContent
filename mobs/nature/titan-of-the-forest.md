# Titan of the Forest

> Hostile — Colossal ancient forest titan (World Boss)

## Overview

The **Titan of the Forest** is an ancient colossus of living wood, ancient roots, and primeval bedrock that sleeps buried beneath the world's oldest forests. To travelers above, the forest resting upon its back is indistinguishable from natural terrain—until an axe strikes its crown or a miner breaches its foot, awakening a primeval force of nature.

Formed from colossal ancient roots, soil, and living timber, the Titan stands as the largest living world event in the mod.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Scale:**
  - A monumental, hunched quadrupedal/bipedal colossus measuring **96 to 118 blocks tall**.
  - Features articulated root-strand fingers and thumbs, jointed stone brows, an opening wooden jaw, and living trees growing across its shoulders and crown.
  - A real-size player stands no taller than the Titan's toe knuckle; players can climb vine ladders on its limbs and ride atop its forested crown.
- **Anatomy & Block Palette:**
  - **Core Frame:** 85% living wood, dense mangrove-log roots, moss, vines, and dark oak heartwood; 15% stone boulders, granite knee cops, and deepslate plating.
  - **Beard & Maw:** A massive beard of hanging roots, swinging chains, and thick moss. Its dark maw features carved bone-block teeth and a throat glowing with pale emerald vitality.
  - **The Heart:** Encaged within thick mangrove roots inside its chest cavity is a glowing emerald froglight core that pulses with rhythmic life.
- **The Three Biome Adaptations:**
  - **Oak Forest:** Dark oak heartwood, oak leaves, hanging vines, spore blossoms, and brown bracket mushrooms.
  - **Cherry Grove / Forest:** Cherry wood bark, pink petals drifting from its shoulders, blooming ferns, and glow berries.
  - **Snowy / Old Growth Taiga:** Spruce and pine logs encrusted with packed snow, permafrost boulders, hanging lichen, and cobwebs.
- **Camouflage & Dormant State:**
  - When dormant, every visible block of the Titan swaps seamlessly for cliff-rock, podzol, or soil identical to the surrounding landscape. The crown sits completely flush with the surrounding forest floor or cliff edge.

## Awakening Sequence

The Titan rests until its domain is disturbed (chopping trees on its crown, mining into its foot, or detonating explosives nearby):
1. **The Pulse:** Emerald cracks spiderweb through the soil across a 30-block radius.
2. **The Ignition:** The froglight heart ignites with a deep subterranean hum, followed by the fiery awakening of its eye-slits.
3. **The Eruption:** The Titan tears itself free from the earth or cliff face, hurling hundreds of stone and dirt crust blocks through the air as it rises to its full 100+ block height.

## Combat & Boss Mechanics

### 1. Seismic Ground Pound (Melee Cleave)
Slams its colossal timber fist or foot into the earth:
- Sends a shockwave of actual falling blocks rippling outward in an 18-block cone.
- Deals devastating physical crush damage and launches players into the air.

### 2. Overgrowth Surge (Arena Enclosure)
Channels the forest's primal vitality:
- Causes living oak and mangrove logs to rapidly erupt block-by-block from the ground around players, forming wooden palisades that trap targets in close combat.

### 3. Root Grasp (Area Snare)
Massive woody tendrils reach from the soil, rooting all grounded players within 20 blocks for 4 seconds (axes deal bonus damage to break free).

### 4. Photosynthetic Regeneration
While standing in direct sunlight or natural water sources, the Titan slowly regenerates health, requiring players to target its glowing chest cage.

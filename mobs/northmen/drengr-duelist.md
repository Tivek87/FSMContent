# Drengr Duelist

> Northmen Faction — Agile skirmisher & unarmored blade master (No Magic)

## Overview

The **Drengr Duelist** is a young, fearless Northmen warrior seeking renown and an honorable entry into Valhalla. Eschewing heavy mail and cumbersome shields, the Drengr fights with reckless bravado, wielding an arming sword and a single-edged *seax* fighting knife with lightning speed and counter-parry mastery.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Lithe, highly athletic humanoid build (1.8 blocks tall).
  - Wears a light crimson or saffron woolen tunic belted tightly at the waist, leaving arms bare to display intricate tribal tattoos.
  - No helmet; long braided hair tied back with leather thongs, exposing an eager, battle-ready expression.
- **Dual Weapons:**
  - Right hand: A finely balanced Petersen Type H viking arming sword with an inlaid brass pommel.
  - Left hand: A 1.1-block curved iron *seax* fighting knife held in an ice-pick reverse grip for parrying.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Bounces lightly on the balls of their feet with dynamic, fencing footwork.
  - Performs agile side-steps and ducking dodges when targeted by incoming heavy strikes.

## Abilities & Combat

### 1. Seax Riposte (Counter-Parry)
If the player attacks the Drengr with a basic melee strike:
- The Drengr deflects the blow with his reverse-grip seax, sidestepping instantly and delivering a guaranteed critical counter-stab with his sword.
- **Counterplay:** Use sweep attacks, axes with shield breaks, or ranged weapons to bypass his parry stance.

### 2. Blade Tempest (Twin Flurry)
Dashes forward and delivers a rapid 4-hit scissor slash with sword and seax, applying the **Bleed** debuff.

### 3. Evasive Roll
When struck by ranged arrows or heavy charged strikes, executes an instantaneous forward or lateral combat roll to reset positioning.

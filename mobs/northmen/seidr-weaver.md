# Seidr-Weaver

> Northmen Faction — High-tier fate weaver & ancestral sorceress (High Magic)

## Overview

The **Seidr-Weaver** is a revered yet feared master of *Seiðr*, the ancient, mystical Northmen craft of fate manipulation, spirit-walking, and weather weaving. Dwelling on desolate, wind-scoured clifftops and secluded fjord sanctuaries, she channels the unseen threads of the *Wyrd* to unravel enemy vitality, conjure ancestral specters, and blanket the battlefield in biting rime-storms.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Slender humanoid female frame (1.85 blocks tall), standing unnervingly upright with slow, deliberate, trance-like movements.
  - Hovering approximately 0.1 blocks above the ground, her hem trailing across snow without leaving footprints.
- **Attire & Materials:**
  - Wears a long ceremonial kirtle dyed in midnight-indigo and woven from raw sheep wool, lined with the pelts of white ermine.
  - An elaborate shoulder cape woven entirely from overlapping raven and gyrfalcon feathers that rustle and flutter even when there is no wind.
  - Head is veiled in thin, semi-translucent gossamer linen; beneath the veil, a gilded bronze headdress shaped like intertwining serpents rests upon her brow.
- **Staff of the Spindle:**
  - Carries the *Distaff of Fate*, an iron-banded ashwood distaff (2.1 blocks tall) adorned with hanging silver thimbles, spindle-whorls, and dyed linen cords that glow with faint violet luminescence.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Translucent, shimmering threads of silvery-violet light (*The Threads of Wyrd*) weave through the air between her fingers and nearby allies.
  - Emits subzero breath mist and tiny drifting frost specks; standing near her causes faint auditory echoes of distant chanting.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Twirls her distaff in continuous circular loops while chanting incantations.
  - When attacked, her silhouette briefly splits into three shifting optical echoes before snapping back into place.

## Abilities & Fate Magic

### 1. Threads of Wyrd (Soul Link)
Spins a glowing silver-violet thread between two enemies within 12 blocks:
- **Mechanics:** 50% of all damage taken by one target is mirrored onto the linked partner for 6 seconds.
- **Severing:** The link snaps if targets move more than 10 blocks apart.

### 2. Breath of the Rime-Witch (Ancestral Blizzard)
Raises her distaff toward the sky, summoning a localized blizzard in a 10-block radius:
- Inflicts **Freezing** cold screen effects and **Slowness II** on all non-Northmen entities.
- Extinguishes all open torches, campfires, and fire blocks within the area.

### 3. Spectral Fylgja (Ancestral Guardian)
Summons an ethereal, translucent blue spirit-bear (*Fylgja*) to intercept melee attackers:
- The spirit lasts for 8 seconds, charging hostile players with knocking headbutts before fading into mist.

### 4. Fate Unraveling (Debuff Hex)
Inscribes a curse upon a single target:
- Inverts positive potion effects (e.g., converts Strength into Weakness, Speed into Slowness) for 5 seconds.

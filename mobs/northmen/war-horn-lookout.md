# War-Horn Lookout

> Northmen Faction — Camp sentry & warband signaller (No magic)

## Overview

The **War-Horn Lookout** is the vigilant sentry responsible for camp security and battlefield communications. Positioned high in watchtowers, atop cliff overhangs, or on the prows of raiding longships, their primary duty is not direct combat, but spotting infiltrators and sounding deafening horn calls that mobilize the entire encampment.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Slender, nimble humanoid build standing **1.75 blocks tall**.
  - Wears lightweight wool tunic and leather leggings for silent, rapid climbing.
- **Equipment & The War Horn:**
  - A light conical leather cap with an eye-visor.
  - Carries a short curved hunting bow and a bronze-banded bone hunting knife at the belt.
  - Slung across their chest is the **Gjallar Horn**: a colossal, hollow curved aurochs horn bound in hammered bronze rings, adorned with raven feathers.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Constantly scans the surrounding perimeter with a sweeping head animation.
  - Upon spotting an enemy, immediately drops their bow, raises the heavy war horn with both hands, tilts head back, and blows a deep, echoing alarm blast.

## Abilities & Alarm Mechanics

### 1. Great Horn Blast (Camp Mobilization)
When a hostile target enters line-of-sight:
- Blows a deep, reverberating horn blast heard across a **45-block radius**.
- **The Alert:** Awakens all sleeping vikings in tents, alerts passive guards, and draws all raiders and berserkers within range directly to the player's position.
- **Warband Adrenaline:** All allied Northmen alerted by the horn gain **Speed I** for 10 seconds.

### 2. Warning Flare (Ranged Tag)
Fires a signal arrow coated in pitch that leaves a smoking ember trail, marking the player with the **Glowing** effect for 15 seconds.

### 3. Skirmish Escape
If cornered in melee:
- Drops a cloud of smoke dust, slides down watchtower ladders, and flees toward the nearest group of Berserkers or the Party Leader.

---

## Tactical Strategy for Players

- **Stealth Elimination Priority:** Players infiltrating Northmen camps must eliminate the Lookout with stealth archery before entering the camp perimeter to prevent the entire base from swarming them simultaneously.

# Longship Harpooner

> Northmen Faction — Heavy coastal hunter & tether specialist (No magic)

## Overview

The **Longship Harpooner** is a seasoned coastal hunter seasoned by brutal whale and sea-beast hunts in icy fjords. Armed with a heavy iron whaling harpoon anchored to thick coiled hemp rope, they excel at controlling the battlefield by skewering high-value targets, dragging them out of position, and smashing enemy watercraft.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Tall, sinewy humanoid warrior standing **1.9 blocks tall**.
  - Weatherbeaten skin, salt-stained braided beard, and bare arms wrapped in thick rope calluses and anchor tattoos.
- **Attire & Rigging:**
  - Heavy waterproof sealskin trousers with thick oiled leather apron and walrus-tooth toggle fasteners.
  - Wears a coil of heavy braided hemp rope slung diagonally across the torso.
- **Weapons:**
  - **Great Whaling Harpoon:** A 2.2-block-long ash polearm tipped with a heavy, barbed barbed iron fluke and a trailing rope ring.
  - Carries a broad blubber cleaver at the hip for close-quarters fighting.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Holds the harpoon in a two-handed overhand stance, balancing effortlessly on boat gunwales and narrow camp piers.

## Abilities & Harpoon Mechanics

### 1. Skewer & Drag (The Harpoon Reel)
Aims and hurls the great barbed harpoon with tremendous velocity:
- **Impact:** Deals heavy piercing damage and embeds the barbed fluke into the target.
- **The Reel:** The Harpooner plants their heels and violently hauls the rope, **dragging the hooked player up to 8 blocks toward the Northmen battle line**.
- **Counterplay:** Players can slice the tether with an axe or sword strike to break free before reaching the vikings.

### 2. Hull Breaker (Anti-Boat Strike)
If a player attacks while riding a boat or raft:
- Harpoon strike immediately shatters the wooden boat into planks, plunging the player into freezing water.

### 3. Cleaver Hack (Close Quarters)
When an enemy is reeled into point-blank range, the Harpooner drops the rope and draws the heavy blubber cleaver, delivering brutal chops that inflict Bleeding.

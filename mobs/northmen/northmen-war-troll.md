# Northmen War-Troll

> Northmen Faction — Subjugated mountain giant & siege beast

## Overview

The **Northmen War-Troll** is a massive, subzero subterranean giant captured in the highest glacier caverns and broken to the will of Northmen warlords. Bound in thick iron shackles and forced into battle with heavy whips and war horns, this colossal brute functions as a living battering ram capable of hurling boulders and pulverizing palisades.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - A towering, hunched brute standing **3.6 blocks tall and 2.4 blocks wide**.
  - Long, heavy arms that hang down nearly to the soil; massive, stone-like knuckles and a protruding lower jaw with jagged tusks.
- **Armor & Restraints:**
  - Clad in crude armor made of scavenged ship planks, iron chains, and rusted hull plating bolted directly to leather straps across its shoulders and chest.
  - Heavy iron manacles wrap its wrists and ankles, trailing broken chain links that drag across the ground.
  - An iron visor bar is bolted across its forehead to keep its gaze directed forward.
- **Weapon:**
  - Swings a massive, uprooted pine tree trunk stripped of branches and reinforced with iron studs (3.2 blocks long).
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Dense breath steam billows from its snout with every roar.
  - Every footstep causes a localized dust cloud and screen tremor.

## Abilities & Siege Tactics

### 1. Trunk Sweep
Swings the uprooted pine trunk in a wide 180-degree frontal arc:
- Deals devastating physical crush damage, hurling all struck players 6 blocks backward and shattering shields.

### 2. Glacial Boulder Hurl
Reaches down to rip an enormous boulder from the earth, hurling it up to 18 blocks:
- Explodes upon impact, showering the landing zone with shrapnel and falling blocks.

### 3. Ground Stomp (Quake)
Stamps its heavy foot into the ground, dealing an area-of-effect stagger that knocks nearby enemies off balance.

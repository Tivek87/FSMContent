# Runesmith War-Forger

> Northmen Faction — Heavy arcane crafter & runic frontline buffer (A lot of magic)

## Overview

The **Runesmith War-Forger** is a revered master of thermal metallurgy and primal rune carving. Neither a frail cloaked mystic nor a passive artisan, the War-Forger marches directly into combat clad in soot-stained apron plates, wielding a monumental runic sledgehammer and enchanted forging tongs. By hammering blazing runic sigils into the shields and blades of surrounding allies, they turn standard raiders into blazing or freezing vanguards.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - A burly, wide-set humanoid standing **2.1 blocks tall and 1.2 blocks broad**.
  - Broad hunched shoulders draped in a charred, thick bison-hide leather apron over riveted iron mail.
  - One arm is bare, heavily scarred and covered in glowing orange runic brandings; the other arm is protected by a heavy articulated iron smithing gauntlet up to the shoulder.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:**
  - Soot-blackened iron, glowing orange forge runes, raw leather straps, and an iron half-mask with a single glowing eye-grate.
  - Wears a waist harness supporting miniature anvils, iron tongs, and glowing slag ingots.
- **Weapons:**
  - **Forging Sledge:** A massive square-headed iron war-hammer inlaid with glowing fire and lightning runes on the striking faces.
  - **Ember Tongs:** Held in the off-hand, gripping a perpetually glowing chunk of white-hot runic slag.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Emits floating orange sparks, thick black smoke puffs, and heat shimmer ripples around their body.
  - Footsteps leave faint glowing char marks on grass and wooden camp walkways.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Walks with a rhythmic, heavy tread.
  - When idle in camps, stands before portable field anvils hammering glowing blades.
  - When casting, strikes the hammer against the anvil or ground with an explosive metallic ringing sound and billowing sparks.

## Abilities & Magic Mechanics

### 1. Runic Tempering (Allied Weapon Imbue)
Strikes the weapon of a nearby Viking Raider or Berserker with glowing sparks:
- **Effect:** Imbues the ally's weapon with **Molten Edge** or **Glacial Temper** for 10 seconds.
- Allies' melee attacks ignite targets or apply Slowness. A fiery or icy rune floats above the empowered weapon.

### 2. Anvil Quake (Earth & Fire Slam)
Heaves the massive forging sledge high with both arms and slams it into the earth:
- Sends a rolling fissure of magma and fractured stone 6 blocks forward.
- Knocks players into the air and leaves burning ground in the fissure.

### 3. Slag Volley (Mid-Range Projectile)
Uses the tongs to fling three white-hot runic slag embers at distant enemies:
- Embers burst on impact, dealing fire splash damage and blinding targets for 2 seconds with smoke.

### 4. Molten Ward (Passive Deflection)
Melee strikes against the Runesmith's heavy apron have a 25% chance to shatter sparks into the attacker's face, dealing minor fire retaliation.

## Camp & Raid Role

- Stations near the blacksmithing tents and central campfires of Large and Leader camps.
- Acts as a high-priority mini-support bruiser; must be isolated to prevent them from continuously buffing allied raiders.

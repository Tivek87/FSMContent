# Party Leader

> Northmen Faction — Warband commander & Miniboss

## Overview

The **Party Leader** is a towering, armored commander who directs Northmen raiding parties. Standing substantially taller and broader than regular warriors, they inspire nearby raiders and crush opposing lines with a massive two-handed battle axe.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Imposing build standing **2.5 blocks tall** with wide shoulders.
  - Heavy chainmail reinforced by steel spaulders, bracers, and an articulated mail skirt.
  - Wears a Spangenhelm helmet with an eye guard and aventail (steely brows showing through the eye openings).
  - A thick, braided viking beard spills over their chest plate.
- **The Four Banner Variants:**
  - Mounted onto their back plate is a tall warband banner displaying one of four sacred sigils:
    - **Raven Banner:** Focuses on aggressive flanking charges.
    - **Wolf Banner:** Increases pack speed and bleed chances.
    - **Serpent Banner:** Enhances tactical defenses and resistances.
    - **Axe Banner:** Maximizes raw physical cleave power.
- **Weapon:**
  - Wields a colossal two-handed Danish battle axe featuring extended attack reach (+1 block).

## Abilities

### 1. Inspire (Banner Aura)
Plants the warband banner into the ground, granting friendly Viking Raiders within 8 blocks **Strength I** and **Resistance I**.

### 2. Devastating Slash
Wide horizontal cleave hitting all entities in a frontal arc, dealing heavy damage and **immediately disabling player shields**.

### 3. Charge & Slam
Rushes forward toward a single target:
- **Unblocked:** Target is stunned → followed by an immediate downward axe smash dealing **massive critical damage**.
- **Blocked:** Negates stun, but the player is launched backward with severe knockback.

### 4. Warcry
Unleashes a deafening roar:
- Inflicts **Weakness** on nearby enemies.
- Rallies all Viking Raiders of the same banner within 30 blocks to their position.

### 5. Axe Volley
Throws 3 to 5 throwing axes dealing 1 to 3 hearts each, inflicting Bleed on critical hits.

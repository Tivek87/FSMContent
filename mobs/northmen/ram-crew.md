# Battering Ram Crew

> Northmen Faction — Two-man siege breacher unit (No magic)

## Overview

The **Battering Ram Crew** consists of two heavily armored, synchronized Northmen veterans carrying a massive iron-capped oak tree trunk between them. Deployed during large-scale raids and village assaults, their singular mission is to smash open wooden village gates, barricades, and player-built fortifications with crushing kinetic momentum.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - A synchronized two-man unit linked by a **4-block-long wooden log**:
    - **The Front Runner:** Broad warrior with a nasal helm and forward-facing iron shoulder pauldron.
    - **The Rear Hauler:** Muscular warrior holding the rear timber handles with matching stride.
- **The Battering Ram (Equipment):**
  - A peeled oak log reinforced with iron strapping, carved with protective dragon carvings.
  - The front of the log is capped with a heavy, solid cast-iron ram head shaped like a roaring aurochs or horned boar.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Moves with synchronized, thundering marching steps.
  - Accelerates into a locked-stride charge when approaching doors, gates, or clustered enemy lines.

## Abilities & Siege Mechanics

### 1. Fortification Breaker (Door Shatter)
Charges directly at wooden doors, fence gates, or wooden barricades:
- **Instant Destruction:** Shatters wooden doors and barricades into loose wood items upon impact, blowing open an immediate breach for raiders.

### 2. Ramming Charge (Frontal Trample)
Accelerates into a straight-line charge:
- Deals massive physical crush damage to any entity caught in its forward path.
- Knocks players back **6 blocks** and downs them, disabling raised shields for 4 seconds.

### 3. Separation (Emergency Splitting)
If one of the two crew members is killed:
- The surviving warrior drops the heavy log (which remains on the ground as an obstacle).
- The survivor draws an iron battle axe and transitions into standard Viking Raider combat.

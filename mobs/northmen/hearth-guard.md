# Rune-Etched Hearth-Guard

> Northmen Faction — Fortified camp protector & warder (Mid magic)

## Overview

The **Rune-Etched Hearth-Guard** is an elite veteran sworn to defend the camp's sacred central hearth and treasure hoards. Their skin and armor are inscribed with defensive warding runes (*Elhaz & Tiwaz*) that glow with soft golden radiance when under attack. They specialize in group defense, erecting temporary runic domes that shield surrounding defenders from incoming arrow volleys.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Broad, stalwart humanoid standing **1.9 blocks tall**.
  - Wears an ornate steel nasal helmet with brass inlay and a mail aventail draped over their shoulders.
- **Armor & Runic Inscriptions:**
  - Polished steel scale armor worn over thick wool gambeson.
  - Glowing golden runic lines are etched directly into the scales of their cuirass and along the shafts of their weapons.
- **Weapons:**
  - **Winged Runic Spear:** A heavy steel spear featuring flared side wings (lugged spear) that prevent over-penetration.
  - **Boss-Reinforced Round Shield:** Embossed with a golden sunburst runic wheel.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Golden runic particles drift upward from their armor plates during combat.
  - Emits a warm golden light equivalent to a torch (light level 10) in dark camps.

## Abilities & Magic Mechanics

### 1. Hearth-Rune Dome (Aegis of the Hearth)
Slams the butt of their winged spear into the ground, channeling an expanding **golden runic dome** spanning a 5-block radius:
- Lasts for 6 seconds.
- Deflects 100% of incoming hostile arrows, splash potions, and magical bolts from passing through the dome.
- Friendly Northmen inside the dome gain **Resistance II**.

### 2. Concussive Spear Thrust
Performs a charged spear thrust that releases a golden shockwave cone:
- Deals physical and radiant magic damage.
- Knocks enemies back 4 blocks and interrupts enemy spell channeling.

## Camp & Raid Role

- Dedicated stationary guardian of the camp's supply chests and chieftain's longhouse.
- Counteracts enemy archer ambushes by deploying defensive domes over fellow raiders.

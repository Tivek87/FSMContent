# Skald

> Northmen Faction — Traveling warrior-poet & drum skald

## Overview

The **Skald** is a traveling Northmen poet, historian, and musician who marches alongside raiding parties and longship crews. Neither seeking bloodshed nor shrinking from it, the Skald stamps rhythmic beats into the frozen ground, chanting sagas of fallen champions that fortify the resolve of allied vikings.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Standard humanoid build (1.8 blocks tall) with a practical, sturdy frame.
  - Braided beard adorned with bone beads; wears practical moss-green wool clothing with rust-and-cream embroidered hems.
  - Light armor consisting of hardened studded leather with iron arm rings.
- **Instruments & Gear:**
  - Carries a wooden back harness holding three traditional instruments:
    - **Painted Frame Drum:** Stretched rawhide painted with runic sunburst motifs.
    - **Curved Ox-Horn:** Bound in bronze, blown to signal charges.
    - **End-Blown Bone Flute:** Carved from reindeer antler.
  - Reaches for and plays instruments with two hands, sending runic music particles into the air.

## Combat & Support Behavior

- **War Chants (Support):** Plays rhythmic drum beats during battle, granting **Resistance I** and knockback reduction to all friendly Northmen within 12 blocks.
- **Defensive Shove:** When pressured in melee, uses the heavy rim of the frame drum to deliver a concussive shove before repositioning behind friendly shields.

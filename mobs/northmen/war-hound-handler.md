# War-Hound Handler & Fen-Hound

> Northmen Faction — Beastmaster & armored hunting hound team (Lightly magic)

## Overview

The **War-Hound Handler** commands the vicious **Fen-Hounds**—giant, thick-furred mountain canines bred for tracking, pinning, and tearing down fleeing prey in snowdrifts. While the handler relies on hunting javelins and whips, the hounds wear runic collars enchanted with ancestral beast wards, granting them immunity to fear and magical scent tracking that pierces invisibility.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **The Handler:**
  - Rugged humanoid tracker (1.8 blocks tall) draped in heavy seal and wolf pelts, holding iron leashes in one hand and short hunting javelins in the other.
  - Carries a bone whistle used to direct attack commands.
- **The Fen-Hound (Beast):**
  - A massive, broad-chested wolf-like mastiff standing **1.3 blocks tall and 2.2 blocks long**.
  - Dense gray and brindle winter coat; head and shoulders are fitted with riveted boiled-leather armor studded with iron spikes.
  - **Runic Collar:** A thick iron and carved whalebone collar inscribed with faint glowing red runes (*Laguz & Thurisaz*).
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - The hound's runic collar glows with a dull crimson light in low visibility.
  - Hounds pant with visible cold breath vapor and emit low, rumbling guttural growls.

## Abilities & Teamwork Mechanics

### 1. Scent of the Unseen (Passive Runic Scent)
The Fen-Hound's enchanted collar detects the spiritual scent of nearby living entities:
- **Detects Invisible Players:** Invisibility potions and stealth sneaking are completely ineffective against the hound within 16 blocks.

### 2. Takedown Maul (Pin & Bite)
The Fen-Hound executes a high-speed lunge:
- Pounces onto the player, pinning them to the earth for 2 seconds while tearing with jagged teeth.
- While pinned, the player cannot move and suffers heavy bleeding damage.

### 3. Javelin Volley (Handler Support)
While the hounds harass the target, the Handler hurls flint-tipped javelins from 10 blocks away, dealing high physical damage.

## Camp & Raid Role

- Patrols camp perimeters and forest edges to sniff out stealthy assassins.
- Acts as fast pursuit units during raids to cut off escaping villagers and players.

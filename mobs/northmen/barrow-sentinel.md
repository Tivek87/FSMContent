# Barrow Sentinel

> Northmen Faction — Crypt guardian of ancient burial mounds (Light Magic)

## Overview

The **Barrow Sentinel** is an austere warrior pledged to stand eternal vigil over the ancestral burial mounds (*haugar*) of Northmen kings and jarls. Carrying an antique soul-fire lantern and an ashwood thrusting spear, they wield only subtle, ritualistic frost magic intended to pacify trespassers and uncover those who hide in the shadows.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Lean, solitary humanoid (1.85 blocks tall), draped in a heavy, weather-worn traveling cloak of dark charcoal wool.
  - Silhouette is distinguished by the high arch of his spear and the swinging lantern hanging from a gnarled staff-crook in his off-hand.
- **Attire & Materials:**
  - Antiquated iron lamellar armor pieces laced with rawhide cords over faded woolen tunics.
  - Wears a rounded iron skullcap helmet darkened with charcoal ash.
- **Lantern of Soul-Fire:**
  - Carries a pierced-iron lantern that emits a cold, pale azure glow fueled by ancestral tallow.
- **Weapons:**
  - A 2.6-block ashwood spear tipped with an elongated leaf-shaped iron head, lightly frosted with subzero rime.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - The lantern illuminates unseen entities, dispelling invisibility within an 8-block sphere.
  - Frost crystals form across the grass or stone near his boots.

## Abilities & Combat

### 1. Chilling Thrust (Frost Spear)
Delivers a fast thrust with extended spear reach:
- Inflicts physical piercing damage and applies **Slowness I** for 4 seconds due to the frozen blade.

### 2. Lantern of Revelation (Passive Glow)
- Any player within 8 blocks of the Barrow Sentinel cannot benefit from Invisibility potions or sneak camouflage; their silhouette is outlined in pale blue spectral light.

### 3. Barrow Grounding
Stamps his spear butt into the soil, creating a 5-block circle of frost that prevents enemies inside from sprinting or leaping for 3 seconds.

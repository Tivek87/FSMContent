# Frost-Tipped Huntsman

> Northmen Faction — Wilderness tracker & frost archer (Lightly magic)

## Overview

The **Frost-Tipped Huntsman** is a seasoned wilderness scout and big-game tracker of the frozen taigas. While lacking the formal spellcasting of shamans, the Huntsman utilizes specialized alchemical knowledge passed down through generations, coating heavy composite arrows in magically frozen brine oil that crystallizes upon contact.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Lean, agile humanoid build standing **1.8 blocks tall**.
  - Wears a hooded hunting cloak of mottled wolf and snow-hare pelts tailored for winter camouflage.
  - Leather bracers wrapped in sinew; soft calfskin boots laced with crampons for cliff climbing.
- **Weapons & Traps:**
  - Carries a massive recurve composite bow carved from yew wood and reinforced with mammoth bone.
  - Wears a hip quiver containing arrows that smoke with cold, blue-white freezing vapor.
  - Carries a bundle of iron-toothed bear traps on their back harness.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - Freezing vapor trails stream behind launched arrows.
  - Pale blue breath plumes exhale from under the fur hood.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Climbs atop watchtowers, boulders, or tents to secure elevated vantage points.
  - Drops to one knee before aiming charged long-range sniper shots.

## Abilities & Combat Mechanics

### 1. Frostbite Arrow (Sniper Shot)
Fires a charged, high-velocity arrow trailing freezing mist:
- Deals sharp piercing damage and applies **Slowness II** and the **Freezing** cold debuff for 4 seconds.
- Pierces through wooden doors and light foliage.

### 2. Glacial Snare (Trap Deployment)
Places an iron bear trap on the ground coated with magical frost oil:
- If a player steps into the trap, it snaps shut: immobilizes the player for 3 seconds and shatters into a 3x3 ice puddle that causes slippery movement.

### 3. Disengaging Leap
If an enemy closes within 4 blocks melee range, the Huntsman kicks off the attacker's chest, leaping 5 blocks backward while firing a point-blank frost arrow.

## Camp & Raid Role

- Manned atop the wooden towers of Leader Camps and the perimeter barricades of Medium and Large camps.
- Accompanies scouting parties as the primary long-range sniper.

# Viking Raider

> Northmen Faction — Standard combat frontline

## Overview

The **Viking Raider** forms the backbone of Northmen raiding parties and defensive garrisons. Equipped for versatile skirmishing, they alternate smoothly between melee axe combat and ranged archery.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Standard humanoid build (1.8 blocks tall).
  - Wears leather wraps, sturdy boots, and a conical steel viking spangenhelm (strictly without horns).
- **Four Tunic & Armor Variants:**
  - **Kirtle:** Woven wool tunic in rust or indigo with embroidered hems.
  - **Fur Collar:** Heavy wolf-pelt collar draped over the shoulders for cold mountain raids.
  - **Mail Hauberk:** Interlocking iron chainmail shirt over padded gambeson.
  - **Warband Tunic:** Dark leather jerkin bearing warband markings.
- **Weapons & Shields:**
  - Carries a round wooden shield painted with **Raven, Wolf, Sun, or Serpent** motifs (slung on the back during archery).
  - Iron bearded axe slung in a leather belt frog; hunting bow and leather quiver with goose-feather fletchings at the hip.

---

## Stats & Baseline Attributes

| Attribute | Value | Details |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| **HP** | Moderate (25 HP / 12.5 hearts) | Standard frontline |
| **Armor** | 8 (Chainmail vest & helm) | Moderate physical protection |
| **Resistance** | Light physical resistance | Resilient against glancing blows |
| **Hostility** | Faction & Banner Based | Defends territory & raids villages |
| **Spawn** | Fortified camps, river longships, village sentinels | Warbands of 3–8 |

---

## Combat Behavior

- **Melee (Preferred):** Approaches with drawn bearded axe and raised round shield, blocking strikes and retaliating with axe chops.
- **Ranged:** Swaps to hunting bow when targets are out of melee range or positioned on elevated terrain.

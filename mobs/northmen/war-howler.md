# War-Howler

> Northmen Faction — Wolf handler & pack commander

## Overview

The **War-Howler** is a feral woodsman and houndmaster of the Northmen warbands. Living alongside vicious arctic tundra wolves, the War-Howler enters battle accompanied by two leashed war-hounds, coordinating flanking maneuvers and tearing down fleeing adversaries.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Lean, athletic humanoid (1.8 blocks tall) with an agile, crouched hunter's posture.
  - Accompanied by **two massive Tundra War-Hounds** (each 1.3 blocks tall with thick white fur and leather armor collars).
- **Attire & Materials:**
  - Clad in tanned wolf-pelts, leather harness straps, and fur-lined hunting moccasins.
  - Face is obscured by a snarling wolf skull worn as a helmet with jaw flaps covering the temples.
- **Weapons:**
  - Wields a barbed hunting spear in one hand and a carved bone horn in the other; carries a coiled rawhide dog lead at the waist.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Whistles and gestures with his spear to direct his hounds; circles targets while his wolves flank from the sides.

## Abilities & Combat

### 1. Pack Command: Pincer Flank
Blows his bone horn, directing both Tundra War-Hounds to break away and sprint behind the player, biting from the rear while the Howler charges frontally.

### 2. Barbed Spear Skewer
Stabs forward with his barbed spear; if the hit connects, the player is slowed by **Slowness I** for 3 seconds as barbs catch on clothing/armor.

### 3. Call of the Tundra (Reinforcement Howl)
If both of his hounds are slain, the War-Howler unleashes a guttural howl that summons 1 additional wild wolf from nearby woods to aid his retreat.

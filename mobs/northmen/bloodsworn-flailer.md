# Blood-Sworn Flailer

> Northmen Faction — Fanatical shocktrooper & chain whirlwind (Lightly magic)

## Overview

The **Blood-Sworn Flailer** is an erratic, fanatical warrior who has consecrated their life to war through a sacred blood-oath. Before battle, they smear their own blood across the spiked iron balls of their heavy dual-headed flail. As they take damage in combat, their blood bond ignites, causing the spinning iron chains to glow with searing heat and deal amplified fire and bleed damage.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Wild, frenetic humanoid warrior standing **1.85 blocks tall**.
  - Hair shaved into a wild dreadlocked topknot bound with copper wire.
- **Attire & Woad Markings:**
  - Leather kilt and iron greaves; torso is bare except for crossed leather bandoliers holding throwing darts and blood phials.
  - Skin is covered in dark crimson woad markings that begin to emit faint red ember particles as health drops.
- **The Weapon (Dual-Headed Flail):**
  - Wields a heavy ash handle connected via thick iron chains to **two spiked iron morningstar spheres**.
  - Chains clink loudly during idle movement; in combat, the spheres spin in blurred circular arcs.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Bobs rhythmically on their toes while idle, spinning the flail head lazily.
  - When attacking, enters a manic spinning sprint, whipping the flail through high arcs.

## Abilities & Combat Mechanics

### 1. Blood Ignition (Passive Enrage)
As the Flailer's health drops, their blood oath intensifies:
- **Below 50% HP:** The flail heads ignite with smoldering embers, adding +3 Fire Damage to all strikes.
- **Below 25% HP:** Gains **Speed II** and permanent knockback immunity.

### 2. Flail Cyclone (Whirlwind Assault)
Spins both flail heads in a full 360-degree whirlwind for 3.5 seconds while moving forward:
- Hits all entities within a 2.5-block radius multiple times.
- Deflects incoming arrows while spinning.
- Each hit inflicts heavy Bleeding and knocks the player off balance.

### 3. Chain Wrap (Shield Disarm)
The flail wraps around the edge of a raised player shield:
- Bypasses shield block damage reduction by 50% and pulls the shield aside for 2 seconds.

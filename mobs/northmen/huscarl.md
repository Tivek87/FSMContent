# Huscarl

> Northmen Faction — Elite fortress defender & shield-wall master (No Magic)

## Overview

The **Huscarl** is the quintessential professional soldier of the Northmen nobility. Void of any magical talent, the Huscarl relies entirely on supreme physical conditioning, heavy martial gear, and centuries of shield-wall tradition. Serving as personal bodyguards to Jarls, they are virtually impenetrable from the front.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Broad, stocky, and heavily armored humanoid standing 1.9 blocks tall.
  - Holds a massive, heavy wooden tower shield covering 80% of his front profile.
- **Armor & Attire:**
  - Full knee-length double-layered iron chainmail hauberk over padded wool gambeson.
  - Heavy Gjermundbu-style spectacle helmet with complete iron eyeguards, chainmail aventail covering the neck and chin, and steel greaves.
- **Weapons & Tower Shield:**
  - **The Shield:** A heavy, leather-bound oval tower shield reinforced with iron banding and an oversized iron boss.
  - **The Weapon:** A brutal iron war-hammer (or bearded war-pick) designed specifically to crush enemy armor and break bones.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Moves with methodical, locked-in strides.
  - When attacked, plants the shield base firmly into the dirt, bracing with his shoulder to absorb impacts without flinching.

## Abilities & Martial Prowess

### 1. Shield Wall Stance (Frontal Wall)
When engaged in combat, the Huscarl raises his tower shield:
- Completely negates **100% of incoming physical arrows and frontal melee attacks**.
- Players must flank behind the Huscarl or use explosive/elemental attacks to penetrate his guard.

### 2. Shield Bash & Concussion
Thrusts the heavy iron boss of his shield directly into the target's chest:
- Knocks the target back 4 blocks and inflicts a 1.5-second **Concussion** (severe screen tilt and disorientation).

### 3. Armor-Crushing Hammer Strike
Swings his iron war-pick overhead against blocking opponents:
- **Disables player shields for 5 seconds** and inflicts heavy armor-piercing damage.

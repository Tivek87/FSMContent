# Runebound Champion

> Northmen Faction — Rune-infused heavy greatsword fighter (Mid Magic)

## Overview

The **Runebound Champion** represents the pinnacle of Northmen martial discipline fused with runic mastery. Chosen by elders to undergo painful skin-carving rituals, this warrior's flesh and greatsword are permanently inscribed with glowing Elder Futhark runes. They combine heavy physical swordplay with explosive kinetic discharges.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Imposing, athletic humanoid build standing 2.1 blocks tall with broad shoulders.
  - Wears an open-faced steel nasal helmet with engraved cheekguards and brass rivet borders.
- **Armor & Attire:**
  - High-grade steel ringmail hauberk worn over padded gambeson, reinforced with engraved iron plate vambraces and greaves.
  - A split leather tunic in charcoal black, belted with bronze-cast rune buckles.
- **The Runebound Greatsword:**
  - Wields a colossal two-handed greatsword (2.2 blocks in total length) forged from bog-iron.
  - The blade's fuller features etched runic inscriptions (*Thurisaz* and *Sowilo*) that pulse with blazing golden light during combat.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - His forearms and blade give off golden sparks and heat waves whenever a runic technique is prepared.
  - Glowing blue-gold runic scars are visible along his neck and arms.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Holds the greatsword in an authentic high-guard stance (*Vom Tag*), stepping forward with deliberate, balanced footwork.
  - Sweeps the heavy blade with fluid, kinetic momentum, pivoting seamlessly into secondary strikes.

## Abilities & Combat

### 1. Thurisaz Cleave (Rune Blast)
Channels energy into his greatsword, executing a lunging overhead slash:
- The impact detonates in a kinetic shockwave in front of the blade, fracturing shields and dealing heavy physical and magic damage.

### 2. Sowilo Dash (Lightning Lunge)
Activates the sun-rune on his greaves, surging forward in an instantaneous 6-block linear dash:
- Leaves a trail of sparks; slashes all targets passed through, inflicting a 2-second Stun on unblocked targets.

### 3. Runic Fortitude (Passive Ward)
When falling below 40% HP, the defensive runes on his mail flare brightly:
- Grants temporary **Resistance II** and immunity to knockback for 8 seconds (triggers once per encounter).

# Seithr-Bound Huscarle

> Northmen Faction — Elite royal bodyguard bound to glacial spirits (Mid magic)

## Overview

The **Seithr-Bound Huscarle** is a chosen champion sworn to protect the warband's chieftain. Bound by ancient mountain oaths, their soul is partially fused with the howling glacial spirits of the northern aurora. In combat, their massive greatsword crackles with pale azure light, releasing bursts of freezing wind and spectral frost armor.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Tall, imposing humanoid warrior standing **2.0 blocks tall**.
  - Carries a massive two-handed broadsword (the *Seithr Blade*) slung across their shoulders when idle.
- **Armor & Attire:**
  - Wears fine dark steel ringmail beneath a deep navy-blue wool tunic bordered with silver knotwork.
  - A heavy wolf-fur mantle drapes across their back, dusted with perpetual hoarfrost.
  - A closed spectacled helm with a faceplate carved to resemble a stoic guardian spirit; glowing pale cyan light shines through the eye slits.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - The blade of their broadsword is enveloped in a soft, undulating aurora borealis glow (cyan, teal, and pale violet).
  - Emits icy breath plumes even in temperate biomes; footsteps leave delicate frost patterns.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Fights with disciplined, fluid greatsword forms—sweeping parries followed by lunging overhead cuts.
  - When activating frost armor, crosses arms over their chest as crystalline ice flash-freezes across their mail.

## Abilities & Magic Mechanics

### 1. Aurora Crescent (Mid-Range Wave)
Executes a horizontal greatsword slash, launching a crescent-shaped wave of pale cyan aurora light:
- Travels up to 8 blocks, piercing through targets.
- Deals magic frost damage and applies **Slowness II** for 3 seconds.

### 2. Glacial Aegis (Reactive Ward)
Upon dropping below 50% HP:
- Crystalline permafrost coats the Huscarle's armor, granting +6 temporary armor points and 30% damage resistance for 8 seconds.
- Melee attackers striking the aegis take subzero frost recoil and have their attack speed slowed.

### 3. Vow of Protection (Chieftain Guard)
If a nearby Party Leader takes damage, the Huscarle immediately leaps up to 6 blocks toward the attacker, delivering a lunging downward cleave that disables player shields.

## Camp & Raid Role

- Stations directly outside or inside the command tents of Medium, Large, and Leader camps.
- In raids, marches shoulder-to-shoulder with the Party Leader as their personal vanguard.

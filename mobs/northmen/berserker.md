# Berserker

> Northmen Faction — Elite dual-axe vanguard

## Overview

The **Berserker** is a terrifying frontline warrior of the Northmen faction. Foregoing heavy armor in favor of raw fury, they fight half-naked under wild beast pelts and fall on their enemies with twin bearded axes and relentless aggression.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Muscular, broad-shouldered humanoid build (1.85 blocks tall).
  - Broad shoulders capped with thick fur pauldrons.
  - Bare, battle-scarred torso painted with runic woad markings; leather trousers with fur knee cuffs and iron arm rings.
- **The Four Bear Pelt Variants:**
  - **Brown Bear:** Traditional forest raider pelt.
  - **Black Bear:** Mountain stalker pelt with dark obsidian warpaint.
  - **Polar Bear:** Ice-wastes champion with snowy fur and pale blue warpaint.
  - **The Rogue:** Scratched, tattered pelt with broken chain links; hostile to all factions.
- **Headwear:**
  - The whole bear head is worn as a deep cowl; the snout and teeth cast heavy shadows over their rage-filled, scarred face.
- **Weapons:**
  - Dual-wields heavy iron bearded axes with jagged, bloodstained blades.

---

## Passive: Ignore Pain

Possesses an innate chance upon receiving damage to **completely ignore hit stun and damage flinch**, continuing their attack animation uninterrupted. Poorly timed counterattacks will not halt their swing.

---

## Active Abilities

### 1. Axe Flurry (Ranged Throw)
Hurls 3 to 5 throwing axes in rapid succession toward a distant target:
- Each axe inflicts 1 to 3 hearts of random damage.
- Maximum damage hits (3 hearts) apply a **Bleeding** debuff for several seconds.

### 2. Wild Slash (Melee Combo)
Unleashes a rapid barrage of cross-cutting axe swings:
- Can be blocked, but **the final strike in the sequence immediately disables shields**.
- Hits that land apply light Bleeding; blocked hits do not cause bleed.

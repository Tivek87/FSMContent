# Jarl of the Frozen Fjord

> Northmen Faction — Warlord, clan sovereign & regional Miniboss (Mid Magic)

## Overview

The **Jarl** is the supreme political and military ruler of a Northmen province or naval fleet. Clad in resplendent gilded mail and rich furs, the Jarl commands absolute fealty from raiders, berserkers, and huscarls alike. Wielding an ancestral, frost-forged war-axe imbued with the elemental chill of the northern seas, a confrontation with a Jarl is a supreme test of martial prowess.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Towering, majestic humanoid build standing **2.3 blocks tall**, exuding commanding authority.
  - Draped in an opulent mantle of black sable fur fastened by an oversized gold ring-brooch over his shoulders.
- **Armor & Crown:**
  - Ornate, hand-gilded Spangenhelm featuring polished brass eyebrow arches inlaid with silver knotwork, with full aventail chainmail draping the shoulders.
  - Masterwork tailored steel lamellar armor polished to a mirror finish, bordered with embroidered scarlet silk.
- **The Ancestral Axe (*Fjord-Biter*):**
  - Wields a colossal bearded broadaxe forged from ancient blue steel, with silver wire inlay along the haft and pale cyan frost mist drifting from the razor blade.
- **VFX & Environmental Details:**
  - An aura of cold authority surrounds him; snow flakes swirl around his boots when he walks.
  - Commands his warband with booming, reverberating battle shouts.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Sits proudly upon an elevated carved oak high-seat (*öndvegi*) inside the chief longhouse until provoked.
  - In combat, steps forward with unyielding, rhythmic momentum, pointing his axe to direct huscarl formations.

## Abilities & Sovereign Command

### 1. Sovereign Command (Shield-Wall Rally)
Sounds a blast on his war horn:
- Immediately summons **2 Huscarls** to lock shields in front of him.
- Inspires all Northmen within 16 blocks, granting them **Strength I** and **Resistance I** for 10 seconds.

### 2. Glacial Cleave (*Fjord-Biter*)
Swings his blue-steel axe in an overhead split:
- Cracks the ground in a 6-block linear trench of packed ice.
- Direct hits deal massive physical damage, **immediately breaking shields** and applying **Slowness III** for 4 seconds.

### 3. Jarl's Wrath (Retaliatory Stomp)
When struck 3 consecutive times in melee, slams his heel into the floor, discharging an icy radial shockwave that knocks back attackers by 5 blocks.

### 4. Last Stand of the High-Seat
Upon dropping below 25% HP, the Jarl sheds his fur mantle:
- Attacks 30% faster and gains a glowing cyan frost aura that deals passive contact damage to all adjacent foes.

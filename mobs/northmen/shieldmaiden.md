# Shieldmaiden Shield-Breaker

> Northmen Faction — Frontline siege shocktrooper & shield-sunderer (No magic)

## Overview

The **Shieldmaiden Shield-Breaker** is a fearless, heavily disciplined heavy infantrywoman specialized in shattering enemy formations, barricades, and shield walls. Relying entirely on raw physical power, martial technique, and iron will, she charges ahead of raiding parties to smash through defensive lines with her fortified tower shield and brutal war pick.

---

## Visual Characteristics & In-Game Model

- **Model & Silhouette:**
  - Muscular, athletic female warrior standing **1.85 blocks tall**.
  - Intricate blonde or auburn warrior braids tied back with leather cord, with shaved sides displaying geometric battle scars.
- **Armor & Equipment:**
  - Heavy interlocking chainmail hauberk reinforced with hardened iron lamellar plates over the chest and shoulders.
  - Steel greaves, studded leather gauntlets, and an open nasal helmet showing piercing, focused eyes.
- **Weapons:**
  - **Tower Pavise Shield:** A massive, flat-topped rectangular wooden shield reinforced with riveted iron bands, iron boss, and painted with warband heraldry.
  - **Flanged War Pick:** A short, brutal one-handed war pick designed specifically to puncture heavy plate and split wooden shields.
- **Animations & Behavior:**
  - Advances behind an impenetrable shield stance, lowering her center of gravity.
  - Emits loud battle shouts when initiating charges.

## Abilities & Martial Mechanics

### 1. Tower Shield Wall (Defensive Phalanx)
While holding shield raised:
- Negates 100% of all incoming frontal arrows, crossbow bolts, and light melee strikes.
- Surrounding allied Viking Raiders gain knockback resistance when standing directly behind her.

### 2. Shield Bash (Concussive Stun)
Rushes forward 2 blocks and slams the heavy iron rim of the tower shield into the target:
- Stuns the player for 1.5 seconds and knocks them back 3 blocks.

### 3. Sunder Strike (Guaranteed Shield Break)
Swings her heavy war pick with crushing downward leverage:
- **Direct Hit:** Deals heavy physical damage and ignores 40% of target armor rating.
- **If Blocked by Player:** **Immediately disables the player's shield for 6 seconds** (longer than vanilla axe disable).

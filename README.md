# FSM Mod

An in-depth Minecraft survival & RPG mod focused on a living world, faction warfare, lore-driven quests, and unique player abilities.

---

## Core Philosophy: Why Vanilla Minecraft Falls Short

Vanilla Minecraft survival lacks narrative cohesion. It is an empty sandbox with disconnected features:
- **Everything Has a Reason in FSM:** Every mob, faction, ruined structure, and boss exists for an in-lore reason.
- **Faction Warfare (*Beef & Alliances*):** Factions clash, raid, and form truces. Whoever you ally with directly determines your enemies.
- **Your Choice Shapes Your Path:** Your faction choice dictates which questlines you receive, which bosses you fight, and which unique ability trees you unlock.
- **Moral Freedom:** Protect villages as an Accord knight, raid frozen fjords with the Northmen, uncover forbidden cosmic secrets with the Archmage, or ally with the undead/dark forces to watch the world burn.
- **Meaningful Exploration:** True lore hunting with decipherable riddle books, compass pointers, and hidden boss arenas.

## Directory Structure

| Directory | Contents |
|-----------|----------|
| `factions/` | Faction overviews, diplomacy, inter-faction rivalries, and reputation systems |
| `mobs/` | Full mob catalog categorized by faction and element (`northmen`, `hearthbound-accord`, `elemental`, `nature`, `neutral`) |
| `structures/` | World generation structures (villages, fortified camps, laboratories, elemental rifts) |
| `systems/` | Gameplay mechanics (Elemental Infusion, Elemental Overload) |
| `lore/` | Overarching lore, storylines, and boss progression |
| `items/` | Custom weapons, artifacts, drops, and crafting recipes |
| `docs/` | AI assistant changelogs and status tracking |

## Factions at a Glance

- **Northmen:** Fierce warriors in snowy biomes who build custom villages, launch raiding parties, and wield ancient rune magic.
- **Hearthbound Accord:** Disciplined knights and footmen defending villages and maintaining civilized order.
- **Archmage Faction:** Rogue wizards, unstable elemental beasts, and cosmic/void horrors spawned from reckless experiments.
- **The Undead / Dark Forces:** Necromantic threats for players who reject order and embrace destruction.

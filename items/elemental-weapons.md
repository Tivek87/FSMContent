# Elemental Weapons

> Endgame armaments forged from primal elemental components

## Overview

**Elemental Weapons** represent the pinnacle reward of the elemental progression path. By collecting the rare Elemental Rods dropped by [Elemental Blazes](../mobs/elemental/elemental-blaze.md), players can forge specialized weaponry capable of channeling multiple elemental disciplines.

---

## Component Rods

Harvested exclusively from Elemental Blazes (1 random drop per kill, equal probability):

| Component | Source Element | Attribute |
|-----------|----------------|-----------|
| **Fire Rod** | Fire | Incineration and burning terrain |
| **Ice Rod** | Ice | Freezing cold and movement impairment |
| **Earth Rod** | Earth | Concussive impact and shield sundering |
| **Wind Rod** | Wind | Airborne launch and defensive deflection |
| **Lightning Rod** | Lightning | Chain discharge and status disruption |

---

## Forging & Requirements

- Requires all **5 elemental rods**.
- Forged upon an [Elemental Infusion Altar](../systems/elemental-infusion.md).
- May require maxing trade standing with a resident village [Apprentice Wizard](../mobs/neutral/apprentice-wizard.md) to obtain the binding catalyst.

---

## Weapon Capabilities

Equipping an Elemental Weapon grants selectable or cycling ability modes:
- **Flaming Cleave:** Sets ground ablaze in front of the wielder.
- **Frost Ray:** Freezes targets in place for tactical spacing.
- **Tectonic Slam:** Erupts stone spikes from the earth.
- **Gale Thrust:** Knocks charging enemies backward.
- **Chain Arc:** Shocks multiple clustered foes simultaneously.

---

## Design Philosophy

- Earned through deliberate exploration, combat, and crafting rather than random loot tables.
- Provides deep combat utility across diverse faction skirmishes.

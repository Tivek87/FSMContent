## Mandatory Design Principles

### 1. Visual Description Mandatory
Every design file (mobs, weapons, structures, bosses, etc.) MUST include a clear and highly detailed description of what it looks like in-game:
- **Model & Silhouette:** Shape, exact dimensions (in Minecraft blocks: height, width, length), stance (quadruped, floating, bipedal, amorphous), and proportions.
- **Materials, Colors & Textures:** Specific block/material references (e.g. deepslate, obsidian, permafrost, basalt, copper), color palette, and texture details (cracked, polished, glowing, rough).
- **Effects & Details (VFX):** Light-emitting elements, particle effects (flames, frost crystals, sparks, smoke, runes, cosmic nebulae), and distinct anatomical features.
- **Animations & Behavior:** How the entity moves, floats, breathes, reacts when idle, and how it transforms during attacks or phase transitions.

### 2. Multi-Elemental Design
Elemental entities should generally not be restricted to a single raw element. Like the Elemental Blaze and Unstable Golem, they combine multiple elements simultaneously (fire + ice, earth + lightning + wind, etc.) or dynamically cycle through elemental states.

# Archmage Questline

> High-tier questline — Source of all elemental mobs

## The Story

The Archmage was once the greatest wizard of the villagers — their protector and the leader of all magic. But an accident (or experiment gone wrong) drove him insane. He fled civilization and began obsessively experimenting with elemental magic, creating twisted creatures in his pursuit of power.

His experiments produced increasingly unstable creations:

- **Elemental Blazes** — His most stable and controlled creations, stationed as guardians in caves
- **Elemental Wraiths** — Failed experiments that couldn't hold their form, now chaotic ghosts of mixed elements roaming at night
- **Elemental Unstable Golems** — Powerful but falling apart, barely held together by raw elemental force
- **Corrupted Mages** — Former students or followers twisted by the Archmage's influence, now serving as his regular forces

The Apprentice Wizards in villages are what remain of his original teachings — students who never followed him into madness.

## The Archmage's Faction

The Archmage's forces are **very rare** to encounter. But when you do find them, expect a serious fight. His faction includes:

| Mob | Role | Rarity |
|-----|------|--------|
| Elemental Blaze | Cave guardian, elemental miniboss | Rare |
| Elemental Wraith | Nighttime roamer, chaotic attacker | Very rare |
| Elemental Unstable Golem | Heavy hitter, cave/mountain miniboss | Rare |
| Corrupted Mage | Regular troops, magic users | Uncommon (near Archmage locations) |

## Void Magic — The Consequence of Too Many Experiments

The Archmage's endless experiments pushed elemental magic beyond its limits. When elemental instability gets extreme enough, it doesn't just break things — it **tears reality itself**. This produces:

- **Void-touched enemies** — Some corrupted mages or elemental creatures develop void/cosmic powers from exposure to too many failed experiments
- **Elemental Rifts** — Extremely rare locations where elemental magic is so concentrated it rips open a tear in reality (see [Elemental Rifts](../structures/elemental/elemental-rift.md))

Void magic represents the final, most dangerous tier of the Archmage's legacy.

## Quest Steps

1. **Collect Elemental Rods** — Farm Elemental Blazes in caves for all rod types
2. **Find Lore Clues** — Elemental mobs have a chance to drop lore books with riddles
3. **Use Elemental Trackers** — Throw elemental items (like Ender Eyes) to find the Archmage's hidden location
4. **Discover the Structure** — Find the Archmage's hidden arena/laboratory
5. **Offer Items to Unlock** — Use gathered elemental materials to open the entrance
6. **Fight Through the Dungeon** — Battle corrupted mages and elemental creatures in his lair
7. **Defeat the Archmage** — Final boss fight with powerful elemental magic

## The Archmage Boss (Concept)

- Uses all elements in combat (fire, ice, lightning, earth, wind)
- May use void/cosmic magic in later phases
- High difficulty — designed for endgame gear
- Arena-style fight in a custom-built structure

## Rewards

- Endgame loot and unique weapons
- Custom abilities
- More lore revealing the full story
- Possible connection: maxing an Apprentice Wizard's trades may be needed to craft the final Elemental Weapon

## Design Notes

- This is the hardest questline in the mod
- The Archmage ties together all elemental mobs into one coherent story
- His faction is rare but every encounter is intense
- Players should feel like they're uncovering a mystery, not just grinding
- The void magic layer adds depth and a sense of escalating danger

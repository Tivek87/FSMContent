# Corruption Questline

> Lower-tier questline — The fallen knights

## The Story

Once noble protectors, certain knights were corrupted by a dark force. They now wander the world as Fallen Knights, hostile and dangerous. The source of their corruption can be traced back to a dark castle hidden somewhere in the world.

## Quest Steps

1. **Encounter Fallen Knights** — Fight them in the wild
2. **Find Lore Books** — Small chance to drop a book with lore and a riddle pointing to a location
3. **Follow the Trail** — Find the location described in the lore
4. **Discover the Story** — Learn how the knights were corrupted and find clues to the source
5. **Locate the Castle** — Find the corrupted castle structure
6. **Gather Unlock Items** — Collect what's needed to open the castle
7. **Fight Through** — Battle corrupted enemies in the castle
8. **Defeat the Corruption Boss** — A corrupted knight boss with dark magic abilities

## Rewards

- Good loot (below Archmage tier)
- Custom weapons and abilities themed around corruption/dark magic
- More lore expanding the world's story

## Design Notes

- Easier than the Archmage questline — a good mid-game goal
- Introduces players to the quest/lore system before tackling harder questlines
- The castle structure should feel ominous and different from normal Minecraft builds

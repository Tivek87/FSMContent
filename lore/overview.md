# FSM Mod — Lore & Design Philosophy

## Core Philosophy: Why Vanilla Minecraft Falls Short

Vanilla Minecraft survival lacks depth and narrative stakes. It functions as an unstructured sandbox filled with arbitrary components:
- Structures and dungeons have no inhabitants with clear motivations.
- Bosses like the Ender Dragon and Wither exist solely as hollow checklist objectives.
- Character progression terminates upon obtaining diamond or netherite gear, leaving exploration without gameplay purpose.

**FSM fundamentally redesigns survival progression:**
1. **Everything Has a Purpose:** Every creature, faction, dungeon ruin, and boss possesses an in-lore origin and an active objective.
2. **Active Lore Hunting:** Exploration yields tangible secrets. Players uncover riddle tomes, lost journals, and ancient tablets leading to hidden trial grounds.
3. **Progression Beyond Gear:** Advancement is measured through unlocked elemental and faction ability trees, renown standings, and world-shaping consequences.
4. **Freedom of Morality:** Players are never coerced into righteousness. Aligning with chaotic entities, corrupt covens, or undead legions to conquer civilized realms is fully supported.

## The Lore Gameplay Loop

```
[Mob Hunt / Exploration]
          │
          ▼
[Rare Lore Tome Drop / Cryptic Clue]
          │
          ▼
[Deciphering Coordinates / Pointer Relics]
          │
          ▼
[Gathering Keys / Offering Rituals]
          │
          ▼
[Custom Dungeon Assault & Boss Encounter]
          │
          ▼
[Unlocking Mythic Lore, Abilities & Artifacts]
```

1. **Uncovering Clues:** Enemies such as fallen knights or elemental anomalies have small chances to drop riddle books.
2. **Deciphering Riddles:** Inscribed texts hint at specific biomes, celestial conditions, or forgotten ruins.
3. **Tracking Mechanisms:** Craftable elemental pointers (similar to Eyes of Ender) guide players toward hidden laboratories or sunken keeps.
4. **Breaching Arenas:** Locked sanctums require specific elemental reagents or sacrifices to unlock custom boss arenas.
